1️⃣ What Is a Scale?

A SCALE relates drawing size to real size. Scale 1:100 means 1 unit on drawing = 100 units in real life.

Practice:

  1. Scale 1:100. Drawing 5 cm = real ? cm
  2. Scale 1 in = 10 ft. Drawing 4 in = real ? ft
  3. Scale 1:50. Real 300 cm = drawing ? cm

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 500 cm (2) 40 ft (3) 6 cm

2️⃣ Map Scales

Map: 1 in = 5 miles.

Practice:

  1. Cities 3 in apart on map. Real distance?
  2. Real 40 mi = ? in on map
  3. Real 25 mi = ? in on map

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 15 mi (2) 8 in (3) 5 in

3️⃣ Blueprint Scales

Blueprint 1/4 in = 1 ft.

Practice:

  1. Wall on blueprint 3 in. Real length?
  2. Real room 20 ft long. Blueprint length?
  3. Real 8 ft high. Blueprint height?

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 12 ft (2) 5 in (3) 2 in

4️⃣ Scale Factor

If drawing is scaled up 3× (enlarged), all lengths × 3, but AREA × 9 (3²), VOLUME × 27 (3³).

Practice:

  1. Scale factor 2 → area factor?
  2. Scale factor 3 → volume factor?
  3. A 4×5 rectangle is scaled by 2. New dimensions? New area?

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) 4× (2) 27× (3) 8×10 = 80, vs original 20 → 4×
