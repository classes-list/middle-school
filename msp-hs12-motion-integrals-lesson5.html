📘 How It Works

Key idea: Start + ∫(rate in − rate out).

Amount at time T = starting amount + ∫₀ᵀ (rate in − rate out) dt. Integrate each rate separately, subtract, then add to the start.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
A tank holds 140 liters. Water flows in at 9 L/min and drains out at 2t L/min. How many liters are in the tank at t = 2 min?
Answer: 154
✏️ Worked Example 2
A tank holds 20 liters. Water flows in at 3 L/min and drains out at t L/min. How many liters are in the tank at t = 4 min?
Answer: 24
