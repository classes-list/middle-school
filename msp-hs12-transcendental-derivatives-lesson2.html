📘 How It Works

Key idea: aˣ → aˣ ln a; log_b x → 1/(x ln b).

For aˣ, the derivative is aˣ · ln a (compute ln a on the calculator). For log_b x, the derivative is 1/(x · ln b). When the base is e, ln e = 1, which is why eˣ and ln x have the simplest rules.
