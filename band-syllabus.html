🎺 Course Outline & Objectives · Grade 6

Throughout the school year, students will learn the value of music by understanding the elements of music: rhythm, dynamics, melody, harmony, tone color, and texture. By the end of the year, all students will demonstrate proper posture, playing position, intonation, fingerings, style, balance and blending, articulation, and phrasing.

Students will use this knowledge and the fundamentals of their instrument to actively listen, express themselves, and engage through music. Music Education allows students to develop a love of music that will carry on for the rest of their lives.

🎼 Required Materials

All students are required to have the following in their possession at every class session:

  • Essential Elements Method Book 1
  • Assigned Instrument (Percussionists will bring book and their own drumsticks to class)
  • Instrument Accessories — 3 extra reeds, valve oil, mouthpiece, cork grease, neck strap, slide cream, cleaning cloth, polishing cloth

Failure to bring the required materials will result in reduced participation points for that class.

📊 Grading Policy

Students will earn grades through class participation, testing, and weekly at-home practice. Students needing extra help may schedule a meeting during recess or lunch recess. All assignments will be posted on Google Classroom — 6th Band. All grades will be posted in Beehively (including late and missing assignments).

*Students and parents should check Google Classroom and Beehively frequently for all assignments and current grades.

Class Participation 50%
  • Bring instrument and book to every class
  • Bring all other required materials
  • Following rules and directions
  • Active participation
Testing 30%
  • Playing and written tests
Practice Logs 20%
  • Weekly practice is necessary to review/remember fingerings and concepts learned during class. Practicing several times a week with shorter sessions is recommended to achieve the 60-minute weekly total and maximize retention.
  • Example: 4× a week (M, W, F, Sa) for 15 minutes each session.
  • The log must include a parent/guardian signature to receive full credit.
  • Due by 5:00 pm every Friday on Google Classroom.
  • Late logs will only receive partial credit.
  • Logs will NOT be accepted 7 days past the due date.
