📘 How It Works

Key idea: F(b) − F(a).

FTC: ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = F(b) − F(a), where F is any antiderivative. Find F, plug in the top, plug in the bottom, subtract.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Evaluate ∫ from 1 to 4 of (−x² + 2x + 4) dx (decimal).
Answer: 6
✏️ Worked Example 2

Answer: 18
