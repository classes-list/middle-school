📘 How It Works

Key idea: y = a·bˣ.

In y = a·bˣ, a is the starting value (when x = 0) and b is the multiplier each step. Evaluate by computing bˣ first, then multiplying by a.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Evaluate y = 2·(2)^x when x = 4.
Answer: 32
✏️ Worked Example 2
Evaluate y = 3·(0.5)^x when x = 2.
Answer: 0.75
