1️⃣ Half-Life of a Drug

Each half-life, HALF the drug is eliminated. Similar to radioactive decay.

Practice:

  1. Start 400 mg, half-life 4 hr. After 4 hr? After 8 hr? After 12 hr?

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 200 mg; 100 mg; 50 mg

3️⃣ Dosing Intervals

Take a dose every half-life = concentration stays roughly steady (steady-state).

Practice:

  1. Drug HL 6 hr. Dosing interval to maintain steady?
  2. Drug HL 12 hr. Interval?
  3. Why don't you take a whole day's dose at once?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) Every 6 hr (2) Every 12 hr (3) Would overdose (spike too high) and wear off before next dose

4️⃣ Real Examples

Ibuprofen HL ~2 hr; caffeine ~5 hr; nicotine ~2 hr.

Practice:

  1. 100 mg caffeine at 8am. Amount left at 1pm (5 hr, HL 5 hr)?
  2. 200 mg ibuprofen at 10am. Amount at 12pm (HL 2 hr)?
  3. 1 hr after taking, amount is 3/4 of original. What is half-life?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) 50 mg (2) 100 mg (3) About 2.4 hr (log calculation) — for this class, roughly 2-3 hr
