📚 Reading Passage

E-Books vs. Paper Books
Some people love reading on tablets. They say e-books are the best because you can carry a whole library in one small device. E-books also let you change the size of the letters, which helps people who have trouble seeing small print. Other people prefer paper books. They say paper books are better because there is no battery to run out. You can also share a paper book with a friend, and you can find your place easily by folding the corner. In truth, both kinds have advantages and disadvantages. What matters most is that you read something -- every day, whenever you can.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

Name one thing e-books and paper books have in common.CompareBoth are ways to read books.
Name one difference between e-books and paper books.ContrastE-books need a battery; paper books don't. Or: e-books can change letter size; paper books can't.
Is 'Paper books are better' a fact or an opinion?Fact vs. OpinionAn opinion -- 'better' depends on what someone thinks.

📐 Language & Grammar

Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

Use comparatives to compare 2 things (add -er or use more).
Use superlatives for 3 or more (add -est or use most).

• short → shorter → shortest
• small → smaller → smallest
• interesting → more interesting → most interesting
• good → better → best   (irregular)
• bad → worse → worst   (irregular)

Try It:
  1. This book is _____ (short) than that one.
  2. She is the _____ (kind) person in our class.
  3. Math is _____ (interesting) than I thought.
  4. Recess is the _____ (good) part of the day.

✏️ Writing

Write an opinion piece: Which do you prefer -- e-books or paper books? Support your opinion with at least 2 reasons.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Fill in the frame: 'My favorite kind of book is _____ because _____ and _____.'

Grade 6

Write a 4-sentence opinion paragraph: opinion + 2 reasons + closing sentence.

Grade 7-8

Write a 5-paragraph opinion essay: introduction, three reason paragraphs, and a strong closing that restates your opinion.
