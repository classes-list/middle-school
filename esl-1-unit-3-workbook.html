📚 Reading Passage

The Clever Mouse and the Rope
Long ago, a small brown mouse named Lani lived in a big banyan tree. The tree gave her food and shade, and Lani was happy. One day, a hungry hawk perched on the highest branch. Lani could not leave her home to look for food. She was afraid. Lani had an idea. She chewed a long rope from a coconut husk, then tied one end to a branch and dropped the other end down the trunk. When the hawk was not looking, Lani slid down the rope like a tiny hero. She gathered seeds all afternoon and climbed back up before dark. From that day on, the other animals of the banyan tree called her Lani the Clever.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

Where does the story take place?SettingIn a big banyan tree, long ago.
What is the main idea of this story?Main IdeaA clever mouse solves a problem using her own idea.
Name two details that show Lani was clever.DetailsShe thought of the rope idea; she used a coconut husk; she waited until the hawk wasn't looking.

📐 Language & Grammar

Past-Tense Verbs (Regular & Common Irregular)

To tell about something that already happened, use the past tense.

Regular past-tense verbs add -ed: walk → walked, jump → jumped.
Common irregular past-tense verbs must be learned: go → went, see → saw, have → had, is/are → was/were, come → came, run → ran, eat → ate.

Try It:
  1. Yesterday I _____ (walk) to school.
  2. Last week we _____ (see) a movie.
  3. She _____ (have) breakfast at seven.
  4. They _____ (run) fast in the race.

✏️ Writing

Write a story retell of 'The Clever Mouse and the Rope.'

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Draw a 3-box story map: Beginning · Middle · End. Write one sentence in each box.

Grade 6

Write a 4-sentence paragraph retelling the story. Include character, setting, problem, and solution.

Grade 7-8

Write a full paragraph retell with a topic sentence, 4-5 detail sentences, and one closing sentence about the lesson of the story.
