📘 How It Works

Key idea: Slope of the secant line.

Average rate of change = [f(b) − f(a)]/(b − a): the slope of the secant line between two points.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = x² + 2. Find the average rate of change on [-3, -1].
Answer: -4
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = −x² + 2x + 1. Find the average rate of change on [-1, 2].
Answer: 1
