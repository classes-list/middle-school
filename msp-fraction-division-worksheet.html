5️⃣ Word Problems

Fraction division shows up in cooking, sharing, and measurement.

Practice:

  1. A pizza is cut into 8 slices. Each slice is 1/8 of the pizza. If Ali eats 1/2 of the pizza, how many slices did he eat?
  2. A rope is 3/4 m long. You cut it into pieces of 1/8 m each. How many pieces?
  3. A recipe calls for 1/2 cup sugar. You have 3 cups. How many batches can you make?
  4. You have 6 m of ribbon and each bow uses 3/4 m. How many bows can you make?
  5. A jug holds 2 1/2 L. You pour it into 1/4 L cups. How many cups?
—

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) 1/2 ÷ 1/8 = 4 slices | (2) 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 6 pieces | (3) 3 ÷ 1/2 = 6 batches | (4) 6 ÷ 3/4 = 8 bows | (5) 5/2 ÷ 1/4 = 10 cups
