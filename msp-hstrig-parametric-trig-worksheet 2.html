1️⃣ Points on a Curve

Plug t into both equations.

Practice:

  1. x(t) = −2t + 2, y(t) = 2t² − t − 2. Find the y-coordinate of the point when t = 2.
  2. x = 6 cos t, y = 5 sin t. Find x when t = 120° (nearest hundredth).
  3. x(t) = 3t − 4, y(t) = −t² − 3. Find the y-coordinate of the point when t = −1.
  4. x = 3 cos t, y = 5 sin t. Find x when t = 90° (nearest hundredth).

2️⃣ Eliminating the Parameter

Substitute, or use cos²t + sin²t = 1.

Practice:

  1. x = 2t + 4, y = 4t. Eliminate t. What is y when x = 2?
  2. x = 2t + 5, y = t². After eliminating t, find y when x = 5.
  3. x = t, y = t². After eliminating t, find y when x = −3.
  4. x = t − 4, y = 2t + 1. Eliminate t. What is y when x = −4?

Answer Key — Section 2

3️⃣ Circles & Ellipses

x = h + a cos t, y = k + b sin t.

Practice:

  2. x = 3 + 8 cos t, y = 3 + 8 sin t. What is the vertical radius (semi-axis) of this ellipse?
  3. x = −1 + 2 cos t, y = −1 + 2 sin t traces a circle. What is the x-coordinate of its center?
  4. x = 7 cos t, y = 3 + 3 sin t. What is the vertical radius (semi-axis) of this ellipse?

4️⃣ Projectile Motion

x = (v cos θ)t, y = h + (v sin θ)t − 16t².

Practice:

  1. A ball is launched at 32 ft/s at 60° from a height of 0 ft: y = 0 + (32 sin 60°)t − 16t². When does it land? (seconds, nearest hundredth)
  2. A ball is launched at 48 ft/s at 45° from a height of 6 ft. How far does it travel horizontally before landing? x = (48 cos 45°)t (feet, nearest tenth)
  3. A ball is launched at 80 ft/s at 30° from a height of 6 ft: y = 6 + (80 sin 30°)t − 16t². When does it land? (seconds, nearest hundredth)
  4. A ball is launched at 80 ft/s at 30° from a height of 6 ft. What is its maximum height? (feet, nearest tenth)
