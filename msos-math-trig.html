📓 Trigonometry — Trigonometry Syllabus

Measuring Triangles, Circles & Waves · Independent accelerated study · Dr. Peter Park · Mary, Star of the Sea School

📘 Course Description

Trigonometry is a full-year course aligned to the Common Core trigonometry standards (G-SRT, F-TF, N-CN, N-VM, and + standards) for accelerated students. It moves from angles and right triangles through the unit circle, trig graphs, inverse functions, identities and equations, the Laws of Sines and Cosines, vectors, polar graphs, complex numbers in trig form, and parametric motion.

🧭 How This Course Works

  • Each unit has daily lessons: Lesson 1 is the unit overview; Lessons 2–5 each teach one skill with two worked examples and practice.
  • Read slowly — every idea is explained step by step.
  • Open the Visual to play with the idea.
  • Do the Worksheet (answers check instantly).
  • Take the Quiz, then the Unit Test (practice first, then graded).
  • Each quarter has a Mid-Quarter and End-of-Quarter test.

📊 Grading

  • Worksheets & practice — 20%
  • Quizzes — 20%
  • Unit tests — 30%
  • Quarter tests — 30%
