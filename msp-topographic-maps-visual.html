🗺️ Topographic Maps & Slope

Drag two hikers between contour lines and watch the slope change.

Big idea: Contour lines connect points at the same elevation. When they are close together, the ground is steep. When they are far apart, the ground is gentle. Slope = rise ÷ run — the ratio of elevation change to horizontal distance.

💡 Read the map: Contours here mark elevation in 20-meter jumps. The center of the hill is the peak. Move A near the center and B far away — the closer to the peak, the higher the elevation.

🧭 Why This Is a Rate

Slope is a rate: meters of elevation per meter of horizontal distance. That's exactly the same kind of quantity as miles per hour or dollars per pound — just rise per run.

  • Slope 0.05 = 5% grade → easy trail (about a shallow driveway)
  • Slope 0.15 = 15% grade → steep but hikeable
  • Slope 0.30+ = 30%+ grade → very steep, use switchbacks or ropes
  • Slope 1.0 = 45° → cliff-like

Try: put both points on the same contour line. Rise = 0, so slope = 0 — flat walk.
