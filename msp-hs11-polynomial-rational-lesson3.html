📘 How It Works

Key idea: Divide out a known zero, then factor.

If you know one zero, divide it out with synthetic division. The quotient is a smaller polynomial — often a quadratic you can factor or solve with the quadratic formula. The number of zeros (with multiplicity) equals the degree.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
-2 is a zero of p(x) = x³ − x² − 4x + 4. What is the largest of the other two zeros?
Answer: 2
✏️ Worked Example 2
