1️⃣ Classifying Triangles

By sides: EQUILATERAL (3 equal), ISOSCELES (2 equal), SCALENE (all different). By angles: ACUTE (all < 90°), RIGHT (one 90°), OBTUSE (one > 90°).

Practice:

  1. 3 sides 5, 5, 5. Type?
  2. Sides 3, 4, 5. Type?
  3. Angles 30, 60, 90. Type?
  4. Angles 60, 60, 60. Type?
  5. Angles 80, 60, 40. Type?

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) Equilateral (2) Scalene (right, since 3²+4²=5²) (3) Right (4) Equilateral (acute) (5) Acute scalene

2️⃣ Angle Sum: 180°

All triangles have interior angles summing to 180°.

Practice:

  1. Two angles are 45° and 60°. Third?
  2. One angle 90°, another 30°. Third?
  3. Equilateral: each angle?
  4. Isosceles: base angles 70° each. Vertex?

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 75° (2) 60° (3) 60° (4) 40°

3️⃣ Triangle Inequality

Sum of any two sides MUST be greater than the third side. Otherwise, no triangle can form.

Practice:

  1. Can 3, 4, 5 form a triangle?
  2. Can 2, 3, 6 form a triangle?
  3. Can 5, 5, 10 form a triangle?
  4. Sides 7 and 10. What range for the third side?

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) Yes (2) No (2+3<6) (3) No (5+5 not > 10) (4) 3 < x < 17

4️⃣ Isosceles Triangle

Two equal sides → two equal angles (base angles).

Practice:

  1. Vertex angle 40°. Base angles?
  2. Base angles 50° each. Vertex?
  3. One angle 100°, isosceles. Other two?

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) 70° each (2) 80° (3) 40° each
