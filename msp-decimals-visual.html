💯 Decimals — Line Up The Point

Set two numbers, pick an operation, watch the place values line up perfectly.

Golden rule: When you add or subtract decimals, line up the decimal points. Tenths under tenths, hundredths under hundredths. When you multiply, count total decimal places in the factors. When you divide, move the decimal point to make a whole-number divisor.

🧠 Big Ideas

Adding & Subtracting: Add zeros so both numbers have the same number of decimal places. Line up the decimal points top-to-bottom. Bring the decimal straight down into the answer.
Multiplying: Ignore the decimal points and multiply as whole numbers. Then count the TOTAL decimal places in both factors and put that many in the answer.
Dividing by a decimal: Move the decimal in the DIVISOR to make it a whole number. Move the decimal in the DIVIDEND the SAME number of places. Then divide normally.
