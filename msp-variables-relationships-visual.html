📊 One Relationship, Four Ways to See It

Change the independent variable — watch the dependent variable respond.

The visual truth: The independent variable (x) is the one you control. The dependent variable (y) responds to it. Words, an equation, a table, and a graph are four views of the same relationship — change one and all four change together.

🧠 Big Ideas

Ask “which one depends on the other?” Money earned depends on hours worked, so hours is independent (x) and money is dependent (y).
Graph rule: The independent variable goes on the horizontal x-axis; the dependent variable goes on the vertical y-axis.
Proportional? A relationship is proportional when y ÷ x is the same every time (the constant of proportionality k). Its equation is y = kx and its graph is a straight line through the origin (0, 0). A starting amount breaks proportionality.
Common mistake: Mixing up the two variables. Time is almost always independent — it keeps moving no matter what.
