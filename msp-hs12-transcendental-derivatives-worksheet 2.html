3️⃣ Derivatives of Inverses

(f⁻¹)′(b) = 1/f′(a) where f(a) = b.

Practice:

  1. f(x) = x³ + 2x + 2 is one-to-one, and f(2) = 14. Find (f⁻¹)′(14) (fraction or decimal).
  2. f(x) = 3x³ + 2x − 1 is one-to-one, and f(−2) = −29. Find (f⁻¹)′(−29) (fraction or decimal).
  3. f(x) = 3x³ + 2x is one-to-one, and f(−1) = −5. Find (f⁻¹)′(−5) (fraction or decimal).
  4. f(x) = x³ + x is one-to-one, and f(0) = 0. Find (f⁻¹)′(0) (fraction or decimal).
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 1/14 (2) 1/38 (3) 1/11 (4) 1/1
