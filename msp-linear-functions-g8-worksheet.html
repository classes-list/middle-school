1️⃣ Slope-Intercept Form

y = mx + b where m = slope, b = y-intercept.

Practice:

  1. Identify m and b: y = 3x + 5
  2. y = -2x + 7
  3. y = x − 4
  4. y = 5x (implicit b = 0)

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) m=3, b=5 (2) m=-2, b=7 (3) m=1, b=-4 (4) m=5, b=0

2️⃣ Graphing y = mx + b

Start at y-intercept (0, b). Use slope to find another point (rise/run).

  1. Graph y = 2x + 1 (start (0,1), slope 2 up 2 right 1)
  2. Graph y = -x + 4

3️⃣ Writing Equations

Given slope and y-intercept, plug into y = mx + b.

Practice:

  1. m = 4, b = -3. Equation?
  2. m = -1, b = 5. Equation?
  3. Slope 2, y-int 0. Equation?

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) y = 4x − 3 (2) y = -x + 5 (3) y = 2x

4️⃣ From Two Points

Find slope with (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁), then use one point to find b.

Practice:

  1. Line through (1,3) and (2,5). Equation?
  2. Line through (0,4) and (2,10). Equation?
  3. Line through (-1,1) and (1,5). Equation?

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) m=2, b=1 → y=2x+1 (2) m=3, b=4 → y=3x+4 (3) m=2, b=3 → y=2x+3
