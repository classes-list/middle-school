📦 Volume — Count The Unit Cubes

Volume is just how many 1×1×1 cubes fit inside a box.

The formula IS the picture: V = length × width × height works because you count cubes by rows, then layers. Change any dimension and the volume changes with it.

🎯 Try It: Build A Box

🧠 Big Ideas

Volume is always CUBED units: cm³, in³, ft³. That's because you multiplied THREE dimensions (length × width × height).
Area is squared, volume is cubed. Area (2D) = length × width → units². Volume (3D) = length × width × height → units³.
Order doesn't matter for multiplication: 4 × 3 × 2 = 3 × 2 × 4 = 24. The box looks the same volume no matter which side you call "length."
