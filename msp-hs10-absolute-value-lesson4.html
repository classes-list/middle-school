📘 How It Works

Key idea: y = a|x − h| + k → vertex (h, k).

y = a|x − h| + k is a V-shape. The vertex is (h, k) — change the sign of the number inside, keep the sign of the number outside. If a is negative the V opens downward; bigger |a| makes it steeper.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
What is the y-coordinate of the vertex of y = 2|x + 5| − 3?
Answer: -3
✏️ Worked Example 2
What is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y = 2|x + 3| + 5?
Answer: -3
