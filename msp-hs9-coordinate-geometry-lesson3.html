📘 How It Works

Key idea: Find b by plugging in a point.

Start with y = mx + b. Plug in the slope and the (x, y) of a point, then solve for b. Parallel lines have the same slope; perpendicular lines have opposite-reciprocal slopes (flip and change the sign).

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
A line with slope -4 passes through (2, -4). Find its y-intercept b.
Answer: 4
✏️ Worked Example 2
Find the y-intercept of the line through (0, -1) that is parallel to y = 0.5x + 1.
Answer: -1
