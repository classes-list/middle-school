1️⃣ Formulas

Cylinder: V = πr²h. Cone: V = ⅓πr²h. Sphere: V = (4/3)πr³.

Practice:

  1. Cylinder r=3, h=10. V?
  2. Cone r=3, h=10. V?
  3. Sphere r=3. V?
  4. Sphere r=6. V?
