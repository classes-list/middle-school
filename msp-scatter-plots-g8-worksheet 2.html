1️⃣ What Is a Scatter Plot?

A SCATTER PLOT displays ordered pairs (x, y) as dots on a coordinate plane. It shows the RELATIONSHIP between two numerical variables — for example, hours studied vs. test score.
Setup: INDEPENDENT variable on x-axis (the one you control or that comes first). DEPENDENT variable on y-axis (the response).

Practice:

  1. In a plot of 'hours studied' vs 'test score', which goes on the x-axis?
  2. In a plot of 'ice cream sales' vs 'temperature', which is independent?
  3. In a plot of 'age' vs 'height of a person from 0-18 yr', which axis is age?
  4. In a plot of 'water depth (m)' vs 'temperature (°C)' — an X-Y plot, which is independent?
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) hours studied (2) temperature (temp drives sales) (3) x-axis (4) water depth

2️⃣ Association / Correlation

The PATTERN of dots tells us the ASSOCIATION between the two variables.

Practice:

  1. Hours studied vs. test score — expected association?
  2. Age of car (years) vs. resale value ($) — association?
  3. Shoe size vs. IQ — expected association?
  4. Height vs. weight — association?
  5. Water depth vs. temperature — association?
PatternWhat it means
PositiveAs x ↑, y ↑ (dots go up left → right)
NegativeAs x ↑, y ↓ (dots go down left → right)
NoneNo clear pattern — random cloud of dots
LinearDots roughly follow a straight line
NonlinearDots follow a curve
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) Positive (2) Negative (3) None (unrelated) (4) Positive (5) Negative (deeper = colder)

3️⃣ Clusters and Outliers

A CLUSTER = group of dots near each other. An OUTLIER = one dot far from the rest.

Practice:

  1. In a scatter of test scores, one student got 95 while others got 40-60. What is that point?
  2. In a plot of adults' heights vs. weights, why might there be TWO clusters?
  3. Should outliers be ignored automatically?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) Outlier (2) Possibly two groups (e.g., male/female heights) (3) NO — investigate first. An outlier can be a real event or a measurement error.

4️⃣ Line of Best Fit (Informal)

A LINE OF BEST FIT is a straight line drawn through the middle of the scatter of dots — as close to as many points as possible. It models the linear trend.
How to draw: Draw a line that (1) goes through the middle of the cluster, (2) has about the same number of dots ABOVE and BELOW it, and (3) matches the overall direction.

Practice:

  1. Should the line pass through EVERY data point?
  2. If most data goes up-right, should the line's slope be positive or negative?
  3. Data: (1,3),(2,5),(3,7),(4,9),(5,11). Best-fit line equation?
  4. Data: (0,10),(1,8),(2,6),(3,4). Best-fit line?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) No — it approximates, doesn't hit every point (2) Positive (3) y = 2x + 1 (fits exactly) (4) y = -2x + 10

5️⃣ Interpreting Slope & y-Intercept in Context

Once you have y = mx + b, INTERPRET slope and y-intercept in the real-world context.
Slope = how much y changes for each 1-unit increase in x. y-intercept = the value of y when x = 0.

Practice:

  1. A model of cost = 10 + 2n (n = # of items). What does slope 2 mean?
  2. Same model. What does 10 (y-intercept) mean?
  3. Test score = 5·hours + 60. What does slope 5 mean?
  4. Same. What does 60 mean?
  5. Height = 2·age + 30 (cm, yr) for young children. What does 30 mean? What does 2 mean?
—

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) Each additional item costs $2 (2) $10 flat/starting fee even for 0 items (3) Each extra hour of studying adds 5 points to the score (4) A student who studied 0 hours would score 60 (5) 30 cm at age 0 (birth); grows 2 cm per year

6️⃣ Using the Line to Make Predictions

Plug an x-value into your equation to predict y.

Practice:

  1. y = 3x + 5. Predict y when x = 10.
  2. cost = 2·items + 3. Predict cost for 15 items.
  3. test score = 4·hours + 60. If you study 8 hours, predicted score?
  4. temperature = -2·depth + 20. Predict temp at depth 8 m.
—

Answer Key — Section 6

(1) 35 (2) $33 (3) 92 (4) 4°C
