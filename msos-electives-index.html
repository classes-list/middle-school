Mary, Star of the Sea School — Hands-on enrichment courses for Middle School (Grades 6–8)

Performing Arts
Mrs. Aczon
This class will explore the basic elements of theater and acting. Through warm-ups, music, and exercises, students will be given tools to develop characters for monologue and scene study. Students will be encouraged to create and role-play stories, and develop voice skills, characterization, memorization, dialogue, improvisation, and scene study. The culmination of this class will be the students’ lead role performances in the upcoming Christmas play.
🎤
Toastmasters
Dr. Eachus
The Middle School Toastmasters Elective is an engaging eight-week program designed to develop students’ public speaking, communication, and leadership skills. Each one-hour session focuses on different aspects of effective communication — speech organization, vocal variety, body language, and active listening. Students participate in fun, interactive activities including impromptu speaking exercises, prepared speeches, and constructive peer evaluations. By the end of the course, students gain confidence in their public speaking abilities, learn to provide and receive constructive feedback, and develop essential skills for future academic and personal success.
🧱
Brick Brains: Lego League
Dr. Vasquez
In this exciting elective, students unleash their creativity and problem-solving skills by constructing various Lego projects. Through hands-on activities, they explore design, engineering, and teamwork. By the end of the course, students have completed unique builds and developed a deeper appreciation for creativity and collaboration.
💬
Chat and Chew
Dr. Freitas
In this elective, students practice the art of conversation around a certain topic, activity, or personal artifact in each class. Whether it is through describing a personal art piece, a chosen work of literature, a recipe, or more, students gain confidence in their ability to articulate their thoughts about an item or concept.
🎨
Art-EASE
Mrs. Taylor
Grades 7 & 8 only
Exploring Art Supplies through Environments. Students explore a variety of art supplies to develop their creative skills, experiment with new techniques, and produce diverse artworks — fostering artistic growth and self-expression in a supportive environment.
🌺
Hula
 
Experience the traditions of Hawaiʻi in our Introduction to Hula elective, open to both boys and girls. Students will learn foundational hula steps, hand motions, and cultural storytelling through Hawaiian music. This class celebrates the spirit of aloha and the beauty of this traditional dance.

How To
Dr. Park
Practical Skills for Real Life. A hands-on elective built around the everyday skills middle schoolers will use at home, on the go, and into adulthood. Across 10 sessions, students learn to do laundry the right way, fix small things around the house, sew on a button, manage their money, cook a simple meal, plan their time, and communicate with adults. Each session includes a short demonstration, hands-on practice, and a take-home “skills card.” Offered twice a year — in Quarter 2 and again in Quarter 4.
