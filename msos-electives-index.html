Electives Classes & Resources

Mary, Star of the Sea School — Hands-on enrichment courses for Middle School (Grades 6–8)

🎨
Art-EASE
Mrs. Taylor
Grades 7 & 8 only
Exploring Art Supplies through Environments. Students will explore a variety of art supplies to develop their creative skills, experiment with new techniques, and produce diverse artworks, fostering artistic growth and self-expression in a supportive environment.
🧱
Brick Brains: Lego League
Dr. Vasquez
In this exciting elective, students unleash their creativity and problem-solving skills by constructing various Lego projects. Through hands-on activities, they explore design, engineering, and teamwork. By the end of the course, students have completed unique builds and developed a deeper appreciation for creativity and collaboration.
🌺
Hula
Dr. Vasquez
Hula Elective is open to both boys and girls. Students will learn foundational hula steps, basic hand motions, rhythm, and stories expressed through Hawaiian music and dance. As students build their skills throughout the semester, they will prepare and perform several Christmas songs, celebrating the holiday season while honoring this cherished Hawaiian tradition.
💬
Chat and Chew
Dr. Freitas
A Chat and Chew elective class provides a relaxed space for students to engage in meaningful conversations while sharing a meal or snacks. Through this elective, students learn valuable communication skills, active listening, and the art of respectful discourse on diverse topics. This class fosters community, empathy, and a deeper understanding of perspectives, encouraging open dialogue in a comfortable and welcoming setting.
🎭
Performing Arts
Mrs. Aczon
Full Semester Elective
This class will explore the basic elements of theater and acting. Through warm-ups, music, and exercises, students will be given tools to develop characters for monologue and scene study. Students will be encouraged to create and role-play stories, and develop voice skills, characterization, memorization, dialogue, improvisation, and scene study. The culmination of this class will be the students’ lead role performances in the upcoming Christmas play.
🥁
Bucket Drumming
Mrs. Nishibun
Grades 7 & 8 only
Calling all wanna-be drummers/percussionists! Turn everyday plastic buckets into high-energy drum kits. In this hands-on elective, 7th- and 8th-graders will explore rhythm, learn foundational stick techniques, and play complex polyrhythms as a tight-knit ensemble. You do not need any music experience to join — just bring your energy, coordination, and readiness to groove to modern beats!
🧰
How To
Dr. Park
How To is a hands-on elective built around the practical skills middle schoolers will use every week — at home, on the go, and into adulthood. Over 10 sessions, students learn to do laundry the right way, fix small household issues, sew on a button, manage their money, cook a simple meal, plan their time, and communicate with adults. Each session focuses on one skill, with hands-on practice, so students walk away ready to do it again on their own. The course is offered twice a year — once in Quarter 2 and again in Quarter 4 — so students can elect it in whichever window fits their schedule.
🎤
Toastmasters
Dr. Eachus
The Middle School Toastmasters Elective is an engaging eight-week program designed to develop students’ public speaking, communication, and leadership skills. Each one-hour session focuses on different aspects of effective communication — speech organization, vocal variety, body language, and active listening. Students participate in fun, interactive activities including impromptu speaking exercises, prepared speeches, and constructive peer evaluations. By the end of the course, students gain confidence in their public speaking abilities, learn to provide and receive constructive feedback, and develop essential skills for future academic and personal success.
🎬
Movies
Mrs. Harrison
In this engaging and age-appropriate elective, students will explore how film has changed — and stayed the same — over the past century. Starting in the 1920s and moving through each decade to today, students will view selected clips or full-length PG-rated movies that reflect the culture, technology, and values of their time. Each week focuses on a different decade, allowing students to appreciate classic storytelling, learn about key moments in film history, and develop their media literacy skills. Guided discussions help students reflect on characters, themes, and the emotional impact of movies — while encouraging them to think critically about the stories they see on screen. This elective is designed to be both fun and educational, with no homework or tests. Students will have opportunities to express their ideas through conversation, creative activities, and optional short reflections.
