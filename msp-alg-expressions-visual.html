🧩 Algebra Tiles

Only matching tiles combine. A positive and a negative of the same tile cancel to zero.

How the tiles work: a tall green bar is one x, a shorter blue bar is one y, and a small orange square is 1. A tile with a red outline is negative. One positive tile and one negative tile of the same kind make a zero pair and cancel.

1️⃣ Combine Like Terms

x y 1 − −1 (negative)

Step 1 — Build each term with tiles (in the order written)

Step 2 — Sort matching tiles into groups (faded tiles are zero pairs that cancel)

2️⃣ Distributive Property — The Area Model

To find a(bx + c), picture a rectangle that is a tall and bx + c wide. Its area is made of two smaller rectangles: a·bx and a·c.
