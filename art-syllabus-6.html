Sixth Grade Art Syllabus

Mrs. Taylor · Mary, Star of the Sea School · SY 2026-2027
Course Overview. Sixth Grade Art explores the history of art through the Art Through the Ages curriculum. Students discover how art has evolved across civilizations while developing technical skills in drawing, painting, printmaking, and mixed media. Each unit introduces students to influential artists, cultures, and artistic movements, encouraging creativity, critical thinking, and personal expression.
Q1

The Ancient World

Focus: Back to School and Art of Early Civilizations
  • Bright Future Self-Portrait
  • Stonehenge Line Drawing
  • Ancient Greek Vase
Q2

The Middle Ages & Renaissance

Focus: Medieval and Renaissance Art
  • Coat of Arms Foam Print
  • Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog Inspired Landscape
Q3

Impressionism & Post-Impressionism

Focus: Color, Light, and Expression
  • Fantastical Japanese Bridge (Impressionism)
  • Van Gogh Irises or Cézanne Still Life
Q4

Modern & Contemporary Art

Focus: Innovation and Personal Expression
  • Le Coq Chalk Drawing
  • Positive Shape Prints
  • Lichtenstein Self-Portrait
  • Basquiat Dinosaur
🌱 Additional seasonal and literacy-based projects are added each quarter to reinforce basic skills and to explore different art techniques.

🌟 Throughout the Year, Students Will Develop:

  • Drawing, painting, printmaking, and mixed media techniques
  • Knowledge of major art periods, artists, and cultures
  • Creative problem-solving and critical thinking
  • Understanding of the Elements and Principles of Art
  • Craftsmanship, observation skills, and artistic confidence
  • Respect for classmates, artwork, and classroom materials
📋 Assessment: Artwork is assessed on participation, effort, craftsmanship, and artistic growth throughout the year. See the grading rubric for more information.

🎨 Middle School Art Class Policy

Students need to come to class prepared with all their materials. Students who come to class unprepared will have a point deducted from their participation grade.

Art supplies include: colored pencils, markers, sharpies, and Chromebook.

Due to the limited amount of time spent in art class, students are expected to use their class time wisely and work diligently on their art assignments in class. Coming to class unprepared will significantly impact the amount of time students will have to work on their assignments.

Assignments: ALL assignments are posted in Google Classroom with their due dates.

Students are expected to complete their art assignments on time. Extensions may be honored depending on the individual situation (Covid, travel, etc.).

Art work will be graded based on effort and time management — NOT artistic ability, as stated in the grading rubric.

If time is used wisely in class, homework is rarely necessary.

⏰ Late Assignment Policy

1 point per day will be deducted. Projects are worth 20 points.

These are calendar days, not "class days" or "cycle days". It is the student’s responsibility to turn in their work on time. I remind students when assignments are due. The number of classes it should take to complete an assignment is listed on the assignment and posted in Google Classroom, and students are told and reminded of the due dates.

Students are welcome to come in after school and, if they pre-arrange time with me, they may also come in at lunch or take their assignments home to complete them. I am available to help every day except Tuesdays.

I will contact parents via email as soon as I can regarding missing/late assignments; however, missing/late assignments also show in Beehively. I will not be chasing students down to find and track down assignments.
