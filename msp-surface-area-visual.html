📦 Surface Area Visual: Unfold the Shape

Surface area = the total area of every face. Unfold the solid into a net and add up the faces.

Big idea: Surface area is how much wrapping paper covers the outside of a 3D shape. Unfold it into a flat net, find the area of each face, and add them all up. Matching colors = matching (equal) faces.

🧠 Formula Summary

Cube: 6 equal squares → SA = 6s²
Rectangular prism: 3 pairs of matching rectangles → SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)
Triangular prism: 2 triangles + 3 rectangles → SA = 2 × (½bh) + (side₁ + side₂ + side₃) × length
Square pyramid: 1 square + 4 triangles → SA = s² + 4 × (½ × s × slant height)
Remember: surface area is measured in square units (units²) because it is an area — not cubic units like volume.
