1️⃣ Vertical Asymptotes

Denominator = 0 → vertical asymptote.

  3. Find the vertical asymptote of y = 1/(x − 8). Give x = ?
  4. Find the vertical asymptote of y = 6/(x − 8). Give x = ?

3️⃣ Holes & Evaluating

Canceled factors make holes.

Practice:

  1. y = (x + 3)(x + 1) / [(x + 3)(x + 5)]. At what x-value is there a hole?
  2. f(x) = (x² + 2)/(x + 2). Find f(-3) (decimal ok).
  3. y = (x + 2)(x + 1) / [(x + 2)(x + 4)]. At what x-value is there a hole?
  4. f(x) = (x² + 6)/(x − 2). Find f(3) (decimal ok).
