2️⃣ Evaluating Expressions

Substitute the value for the variable, then simplify.

Practice:

  1. Evaluate 3x + 4 when x = 5
  2. Evaluate 2x − 1 when x = 8
  3. Evaluate x/4 when x = 24
  4. Evaluate 4x + 2 when x = 3

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 19 (2) 15 (3) 6 (4) 14

3️⃣ Two-Variable Expressions

Substitute each variable.

Practice:

  1. Evaluate 2a + 3b when a = 4, b = 5
  2. Evaluate x + y − 2 when x = 10, y = 7
  3. Evaluate xy when x = 3, y = 6

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 23 (2) 15 (3) 18

4️⃣ Word Problems

Write an expression from words.

Practice:

  1. A book costs $5. Write an expression for the cost of n books.
  2. A car goes 60 mph. Distance in t hours?
  3. A phone plan: $10 flat fee plus $0.05 per text. Cost for x texts?
  4. Sarah is 5 years older than her sister. Sister is y years. Sarah's age?

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) 5n (2) 60t (3) 10 + 0.05x (4) y + 5
