Big idea: Area = the number of unit squares that fit inside a shape. Every formula here comes from the rectangle: base × height. Triangles are half a rectangle, parallelograms rearrange into a rectangle, and trapezoids use the average of the two bases.

🧠 Formula Summary

Rectangle: A = b × h (rows of unit squares)
Parallelogram: A = b × h (cut off the slanted triangle, slide it over → rectangle)
Triangle: A = (b × h) ÷ 2 (exactly half of the rectangle around it)
Trapezoid: A = ((b₁ + b₂) ÷ 2) × h (a rectangle as wide as the average base)
Watch out: the height is always measured straight up (perpendicular to the base) — not along a slanted side.
