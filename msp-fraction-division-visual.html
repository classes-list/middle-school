🥤 Fraction Division — How Many Fit?

Dividing by a fraction means: how many of THIS size fit inside THAT?

Reframe division: 6 ÷ 2 = 3 asks "how many 2s fit in 6?" The answer is 3.
Same idea for fractions: 2 ÷ ⅓ asks "how many ⅓-cups fit in 2 whole cups?" Watch it fill up.

🎯 Try It: How Many Small Cups Fit in the Big One?

How many 13-cups fit in 2 whole cups?
Why "flip and multiply" works: Dividing by ⅓ is the same as multiplying by 3, because there are 3 thirds in every whole. So 2 ÷ ⅓ = 2 × 3 = 6. The trick just does the counting for you.

🧠 The Big Pattern

Dividing by a fraction less than 1 makes the answer BIGGER. That feels wrong at first — but it's true. Smaller pieces mean MORE of them fit inside a whole.
Dividing by a fraction greater than 1 (like 3/2) makes the answer SMALLER, just like whole-number division. The fraction's size vs. 1 tells you which way it goes.
