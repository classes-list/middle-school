📐 Parallel Lines Cut By A Transversal

Eight angles from three lines. Only two unique values.

Two parallel lines + one transversal = 8 angles, but they only come in TWO sizes: an acute value and its supplement (adds to 180°). Corresponding, alternate interior, and alternate exterior angles are all equal.

🎯 Try It: Change The Transversal Angle

Acute value: 60° — angles 1, 4, 5, 8
Obtuse value: 120° — angles 2, 3, 6, 7
All same-color angles are EQUAL. All different-color pairs are SUPPLEMENTARY (add to 180°).

🧠 Angle Names

Corresponding angles (same corner position on each parallel line): 1&5, 2&6, 3&7, 4&8. Equal.
Alternate interior angles (between the parallels, on opposite sides of transversal): 3&5, 4&6. Equal.
Alternate exterior angles (outside the parallels, on opposite sides): 1&7, 2&8. Equal.
Co-interior (same-side interior) angles: 3&6, 4&5. Supplementary (add to 180°).
