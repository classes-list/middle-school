🌟 Our Mission

To provide a Christ-centered education that reflects academic excellence and stewardship, and to provide a learning environment where students can foster confidence and social skills and become lifelong learners.

📚 Academics

  • Imagine Learning — online reading program used throughout the year.
  • Explode the Code — phonics and reinforcement.
  • Spelling Skills — vocabulary development at each student’s level.
  • Language Arts — parts of speech and sentence structure.
  • Reading Skills — Facts, Sequence, Context, Main Idea, Conclusion, and Inference.
  • Writing Skills — personal narratives, informative writing, descriptive writing, opinion and comparative writing, stories, and short reports.

🧭 English as a Second Language — Scope

I. Communication

  • Oral and written communication
  • Understanding directions
  • Listening and following directions

II. Vocabulary & Grammar Usage

  • Parts of speech
  • Writing in complete sentences
  • Mechanics

III. Reading

  • Decoding words
  • Punctuation & intonation
  • Fluency

IV. Comprehension

  • Understanding directions & questions
  • Responding in complete sentences
  • Context clues, Main Idea, details
  • Cause & effect, predicting outcomes
  • Inference & drawing conclusions

V. Writing

  • Sentences, questions, and answers
  • Short answers, paragraphs, essays
  • Different forms of writing

📝 Homework & Support at Home

  • Homework is assigned daily and should take about 20 minutes to complete independently. It is due when the next class is in session.
  • Computers and other tools such as dictionaries are allowed.
  • I encourage the use of English in the home daily.
  • Reading aloud for 20 minutes each day helps students speak English clearly and write in complete sentences.
  • Students who need assistance completing assignments may finish their work during lunch recess in my room.
