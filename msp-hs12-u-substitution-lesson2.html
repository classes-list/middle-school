📘 How It Works

Key idea: u = x² + 1, du = 2x dx.

Let u be the inside expression (like x² + 1). Find du = u′ dx. Replace everything with u, integrate, then substitute back.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
