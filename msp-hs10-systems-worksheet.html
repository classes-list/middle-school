1️⃣ Elimination

Make one variable’s coefficients opposites and add.

Practice:

  1. Solve the system 4x − 3y = 11 and 4x + 4y = 32. What is x?
  2. Solve the system 2x − y = 11 and 3x − y = 14. What is y?
  3. Solve the system x − 3y = 16 and 3x + 2y = -18. What is y?
  4. Solve the system x − 3y = -6 and 4x + 4y = 24. What is x?

2️⃣ Substitution & Word Problems

Replace a variable with its expression.

Practice:

  1. Solve: y = 3x + 5 and 3x + 2y = 10. What is x?
  2. Adult tickets cost $8 and child tickets cost $4. 23 tickets were sold for $116. How many adult tickets were sold?
  3. Solve: y = 3x + 1 and x + 3y = -7. What is x?
  4. Adult tickets cost $11 and child tickets cost $8. 49 tickets were sold for $482. How many adult tickets were sold?

3️⃣ Three Variables

Back-substitute from the simplest equation.

Practice:

  1. Solve: x + y + z = 4, y + z = 1, z = -4. What is z?
  2. Solve: x + y + z = 7, y + z = 3, z = -1. What is x?
  3. Solve: x + y + z = 10, y + z = 5, z = 4. What is z?
  4. Solve: x + y + z = 0, y + z = 4, z = 3. What is x?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) -4 (2) 4 (3) 4 (4) -4

4️⃣ Systems of Inequalities

Practice:

  1. Is the point (4, -4) a solution of y ≤ 0x + 2? (yes or no)
  2. Is the point (1, -2) a solution of y < 3x + 2? (yes or no)
  3. Is the point (-1, 1) a solution of y > x − 3? (yes or no)
  4. Is the point (4, -1) a solution of y ≤ 2x − 1? (yes or no)
