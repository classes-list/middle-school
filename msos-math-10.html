📓 Math 10 — Algebra II Syllabus

Modeling the World with Functions · Independent accelerated study · Dr. Peter Park · Mary, Star of the Sea School

📘 Course Description

Math 10 is a full high-school Algebra II course aligned to the Common Core State Standards (functions, polynomials, rational and radical expressions, exponentials and logarithms, sequences, probability, and trigonometric functions), written for accelerated middle-school students working independently.

🧭 How This Course Works

  • Read each unit page slowly — every idea is explained step by step with worked examples.
  • Open the Visual to play with the idea.
  • Do the Worksheet (answers check instantly).
  • Take the Quiz, then the Unit Test (practice first, then graded).
  • Each quarter has a Mid-Quarter and End-of-Quarter test.

📊 Grading

  • Worksheets & practice — 20%
  • Quizzes — 20%
  • Unit tests — 30%
  • Quarter tests — 30%
