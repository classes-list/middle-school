1️⃣ Points on a Curve

Plug t into both equations.

Practice:

  1. x(t) = 4t + 1, y(t) = t² + 5t + 1. Find the y-coordinate of the point when t = −1.
  2. x(t) = −t + 1, y(t) = −t² − 3t − 3. Find the x-coordinate of the point when t = 3.
  3. x(t) = −4t − 2, y(t) = −2t² + 4t + 3. Find the x-coordinate of the point when t = 0.
  4. x(t) = 2t + 5, y(t) = 2t² − 2t + 5. Find the y-coordinate of the point when t = 3.

Practice:

  3. x = 2t − 3, y = t². After eliminating t, find y when x = −1.

3️⃣ Lines & Circles

Slope = (y-step)/(x-step); circle: center (h, k), radius r.

Practice:

  1. The line x = 1 − 4t, y = −4 + 2t. What is its slope?
  2. x = −3 + 3 cos t, y = 1 + 3 sin t traces a circle. What is the x-coordinate of its center?
  3. The line x = 1 + 3t, y = 5 − t. What is its slope?
  4. x = −4 + cos t, y = −2 + sin t traces a circle. What is the x-coordinate of its center?

4️⃣ Projectile Motion

Horizontal: v cos θ · t. Vertical: h + v sin θ · t − 16t².

Practice:

  1. A ball is launched at 80 ft/s at 45° from a height of 6 ft: y = 6 + (80 sin 45°)t − 16t². When does it land? (seconds, nearest hundredth)
  2. A ball is launched at 32 ft/s at 45° from a height of 0 ft. How far does it travel horizontally before landing? x = (32 cos 45°)t (feet, nearest tenth)
  3. A ball is launched at 32 ft/s at 30° from a height of 6 ft. What is its maximum height? (feet, nearest tenth)
  4. A ball is launched at 32 ft/s at 60° from a height of 6 ft. What is its maximum height? (feet, nearest tenth)
