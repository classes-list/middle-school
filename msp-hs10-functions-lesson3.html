📘 How It Works

Key idea: Slope between two points on the graph.

Average rate of change is the slope between two points on the graph: [f(b) − f(a)] ÷ (b − a). Find both outputs first, subtract them, then divide by the change in x.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Find the average rate of change of f(x) = x³ from x = 2 to x = 3.
Answer: 19
✏️ Worked Example 2
Find the average rate of change of f(x) = x³ from x = 1 to x = 3.
Answer: 13
