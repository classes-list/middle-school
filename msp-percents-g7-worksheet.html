1️⃣ Percent Increase / Decrease

Percent change = |New − Old| ÷ Old × 100. If new > old → increase. If new < old → decrease.
Formula: % change = (change ÷ original) × 100

Practice:

  1. A price rises from $20 to $25. % increase?
  2. A price drops from $50 to $40. % decrease?
  3. Score goes from 60 to 75. % increase?
  4. Population drops from 500 to 450. % decrease?

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 25% (2) 20% (3) 25% (4) 10%
