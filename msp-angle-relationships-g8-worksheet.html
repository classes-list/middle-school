3️⃣ Working with Algebra

If two angles are equal (or supplementary), set up an equation.

Practice:

  1. Two corresponding angles: (2x + 10)° and 50°. Find x.
  2. Two alternate interior: (3x)° and 60°. Find x.
  3. Two co-interior: 70° and (x + 30)°. Find x.

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 2x+10=50 → x=20 (2) 3x=60 → x=20 (3) 70+x+30=180 → x=80

4️⃣ Triangle Angle Sum Revisited
