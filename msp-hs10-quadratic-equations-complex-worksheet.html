1️⃣ Quadratic Formula

x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / (2a).

Practice:

  1. Use the quadratic formula to solve x² + 5x = 0. Give the smaller solution.
  2. Use the quadratic formula to solve x² − 5x − 14 = 0. Give the smaller solution.
  3. Use the quadratic formula to solve 2x² − 10x + 8 = 0. Give the smaller solution.
  4. Use the quadratic formula to solve x² − 2x − 15 = 0. Give the larger solution.

2️⃣ The Discriminant

D = b² − 4ac decides how many real solutions.

Practice:

  1. Find the discriminant of 2x² + 5x − 6 = 0.
  2. How many real solutions does x² − 5x + 3 = 0 have? (0, 1, or 2)
  3. Find the discriminant of x² + 7x − 1 = 0.
  4. How many real solutions does 2x² + 7x + 8 = 0 have? (0, 1, or 2)

3️⃣ Complex Numbers

Multiply like binomials, then i² = −1.

Practice:

  1. Multiply (3 − 1i)(-4 − 1i). What is the imaginary part of the answer?
  2. Solve x² + 64 = 0. The solutions are ±bi. What is b?
  3. Multiply (4 + 3i)(3 − 2i). What is the real part of the answer?
  4. Solve x² + 25 = 0. The solutions are ±bi. What is b?

4️⃣ Powers of i
