📘 How It Works

Key idea: y = a(x − h)² + k → vertex (h, k).

In vertex form y = a(x − h)² + k, you can read the vertex straight off: (h, k). Flip the sign of the number inside the parentheses; keep the sign of k.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
y = -0.5(x + 6)² − 6. What is the y-coordinate of the vertex?
Answer: -6
✏️ Worked Example 2
y = 3(x + 5)² − 6. What is the y-coordinate of the vertex?
Answer: -6
