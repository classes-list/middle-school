📚 Reading Passage

The Missing Lunchbox
Kai reached into his backpack for his lunchbox, but it was not there. He was sure he had packed it that morning. He remembered putting a peanut-butter sandwich, a banana, and a bottle of water inside. Kai frowned. Then he remembered: he had set the lunchbox on the kitchen counter while he tied his shoes. Suddenly, his little sister Lehua giggled at the door of the cafeteria. She was holding the lunchbox in her hands. 'You forgot it again!' she said. 'Mom told me to bring it. That is the second time this week.' Kai grinned. He would have to write himself a note tomorrow morning.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

What does 'frowned' probably mean?ContextMade a sad or upset face.
How did Kai's lunchbox get to school?InferenceHis sister Lehua brought it (their mom asked her to).
Why did Kai forget his lunchbox?Cause & EffectHe put it down to tie his shoes and then forgot it on the counter.
What can you infer about Kai's habits?InferenceHe forgets things a lot -- Lehua says it is the second time this week.

📐 Language & Grammar

Signal Words: Because, So, But, When

Signal words connect ideas and show how they relate.

because / since: gives a reason — I was late because the bus was slow.
so / therefore: gives a result — The bus was slow, so I was late.
but / however: shows a contrast — I ran fast, but I was still late.
when: tells time — I felt worried when the bus was slow.

Try It:
  1. I was hungry _____ I forgot my lunch.
  2. Kai grinned _____ Lehua brought his lunchbox.
  3. Lehua is younger, _____ she remembered.
  4. I write notes _____ I forget things.

✏️ Writing

Write a short response: What can you infer about the relationship between Kai and Lehua? Use text evidence.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Draw Kai and Lehua. Under the drawing, write 2 sentences that tell how they act with each other.

Grade 6

Write 3 sentences: your inference + 2 pieces of text evidence starting with 'The text says...'

Grade 7-8

Write a 5-sentence short response with a topic sentence, 3 pieces of evidence, and a closing sentence.
