📘 How It Works

Key idea: x = (v cos θ)t, y = h + (v sin θ)t − 16t².

Split the launch speed into horizontal (v cos θ) and vertical (v sin θ) parts. Gravity only affects the vertical: y = h + (v sin θ)t − 16t². Set y = 0 for the landing time, then plug into x.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
