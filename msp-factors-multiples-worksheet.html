2️⃣ Prime Factorization

Every whole number can be written as a product of primes (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, …). Use a factor tree.
Example: 60 → 60 = 2 × 30 = 2 × 2 × 15 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 = 2² × 3 × 5

3️⃣ Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

GCF = the largest number that divides evenly into both. Method: prime factorize each, then multiply the shared primes (taking the smallest power).
Example: GCF(24, 36). 24 = 2³ × 3, 36 = 2² × 3². Shared: 2² × 3 = 4 × 3 = 12

4️⃣ Least Common Multiple (LCM)

LCM = the smallest number that BOTH numbers divide into evenly. Method: prime factorize each, take ALL primes (highest power) and multiply.
Example: LCM(4, 6). 4 = 2², 6 = 2 × 3. Take highest of each prime: 2² × 3 = 12

5️⃣ Word Problems (GCF vs LCM)

GCF problems: dividing things into equal groups (largest possible group). LCM problems: when two events happen at the same time (next time they meet).

Practice:

  1. A teacher has 24 pencils and 36 erasers. She wants to make identical goody bags with no leftovers. What is the largest number of bags she can make?
  2. Bus A comes every 12 minutes, Bus B every 18 minutes. If both arrive at 8:00 AM, when do they arrive together next?
  3. Two runners start together. One laps every 3 minutes, the other every 5 minutes. When do they meet at the start again?
  4. A packet of 30 stickers and a packet of 45 stickers. Largest equal-size groups with none left over?

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) GCF(24,36) = 12 bags | (2) LCM(12,18) = 36 min → 8:36 AM | (3) LCM(3,5) = 15 min | (4) GCF(30,45) = 15 stickers per group
