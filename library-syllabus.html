👋 Introduction

Welcome to the start of the 2026–2027 school year! Mary, Star of the Sea School is fortunate to have a beautiful, air-conditioned library with thousands of books — some older, award-winning classics, and others brand-new additions to our growing collection. Our library and its resources are available to all students and parents in grades kindergarten through eighth. Our primary goal is to assist students in obtaining recreational and curriculum-related reading materials and to develop and nurture a love for reading and learning. Students are encouraged to borrow books regularly and are responsible for their safe return.

Each homeroom has pre-scheduled library visits. However, books may be borrowed or returned throughout the school day as their teacher allows.

Mary, Star of the Sea School parents are always welcome in our library and are encouraged to borrow books to read to their children.

💰 Lost or Damaged Books

A replacement fee will be charged for lost or damaged books. Students are asked to either replace the book or cover its cost.

🌟 Library Rules & Expectations

Throughout the first month of school (and as needed thereafter), I introduce our library rules and expectations with each class. Please take the time to review our library rules with your child and return the forms with them.

🛡 Be Safe

  • I will stay in the library and use walking feet.
  • I will respect others’ personal space.

🙏 Be Respectful

  • I will use the appropriate voice level for different activities in the library (silence, whisper, talking voice, presenting voice).
  • I will listen attentively.
  • I will care for books and our library.

✅ Be Responsible

  • I will return my library books on time.
  • I will replace or cover the cost of damaged or lost books.

🤝 Be Cooperative

  • I will help others.
  • I will take turns talking and listening.
  • I will participate in library activities.
