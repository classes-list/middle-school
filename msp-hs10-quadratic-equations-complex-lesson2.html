📘 How It Works

Key idea: x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / (2a).

Put the equation in the form ax² + bx + c = 0 and write down a, b, and c (with their signs). Substitute into x = [−b ± √(b² − 4ac)]/(2a). Compute the discriminant first, then do the + and − versions separately.
