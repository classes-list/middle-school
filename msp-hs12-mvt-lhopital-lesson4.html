📘 How It Works

Key idea: Test critical points and endpoints.

Candidates Test: find all critical points (f′ = 0) inside [a, b]. Evaluate f at those points AND at both endpoints. The largest value is the absolute max; the smallest is the absolute min.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Find the absolute minimum value of f(x) = x³ − 3x on [−2, 1]. (Candidates Test)
Answer: -2
✏️ Worked Example 2
Find the absolute minimum value of f(x) = x³ − 12x on [−3, 4]. (Candidates Test)
