⚖️ y = kx — Everything Scales By The Same Factor

One constant, k, controls the whole relationship. Change it. Watch every row of the table scale together.

The big idea: Two quantities are proportional when one is always the same fixed number times the other. That fixed number is called the constant of proportionality, written k. The equation is y = kx. Every proportional relationship graphs as a straight line through the origin (0, 0).

🔍 Is It Proportional? Two Quick Tests

Test 1 — Table check: Compute y ÷ x for every row. If you get the same number every time, it's proportional and that number is k.
Test 2 — Graph check: A proportional relationship is a straight line through (0, 0). If the line misses the origin, it's NOT proportional.

Watch out: Just because a line is straight does NOT make it proportional. It also must go through (0, 0). y = 2x + 1 is a straight line — but it starts at (0, 1), not the origin. So it's NOT proportional.

🧠 Where You'll See This

Recipes: If a recipe makes 24 cookies with 2 cups of flour, k = 2/24 = 1/12 cup per cookie. Doubling cookies doubles flour — because it's proportional.
Speed: If a car goes 60 mph, distance = 60 · time. That's y = kx with k = 60. Cruising at constant speed is a perfect proportional relationship.
Sales tax: Every $1 spent adds the SAME tax rate. Total tax = rate · price. y = kx with k = tax rate. That's why proportional thinking is the backbone of finance and shopping.
