📘 How It Works

Key idea: Compare the slopes.

Put both lines in y = mx + b form and compare slopes. Same slope → parallel. Slopes multiply to −1 → perpendicular. Otherwise → neither. Watch out: a line written as 2y = 4x + 1 has slope 2, not 4.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Line 1: y = 0.5x − 3. Line 2: y = 1.5x + 0. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
Answer: neither
✏️ Worked Example 2
Line 1: y = -2x + 1. Line 2: y = -1x − 2. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
Answer: neither
