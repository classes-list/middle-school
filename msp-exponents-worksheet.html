1️⃣ Exponent Basics

An exponent tells you how many times to multiply the base by itself. In 5³, base = 5, exponent = 3, meaning 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

2️⃣ Product Rule: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

When multiplying powers with the SAME base, ADD the exponents.
Why? 2³ × 2² = (2·2·2)(2·2) = 2⁵ = 2³⁺².

5️⃣ Zero Exponent: a⁰ = 1

Any nonzero base to the zero power equals 1. (Except 0⁰, which is undefined.)
Why? Because aⁿ ÷ aⁿ = 1 AND aⁿ⁻ⁿ = a⁰. So a⁰ = 1.

6️⃣ Negative Exponents: a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ

A negative exponent means reciprocal. Move the base to the other side of the fraction bar.
Example: 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8. Also: 1/x⁻² = x².
