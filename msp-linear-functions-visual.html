📉 y = mx + b — Slope & y-Intercept

Two knobs control every straight line. Turn them, watch the line move.

Two ingredients per line: m = slope (how tilted), b = y-intercept (where it crosses the y-axis). Change m to tilt. Change b to slide up/down.

🧠 Big Ideas

Slope = rise / run. Positive slope goes UP left-to-right. Negative slope goes DOWN. Zero = flat. Undefined = vertical.
y-intercept (b) is where x = 0. To find it from any linear equation, just plug in x = 0.
Steep vs gentle: Bigger |m| = steeper. m = 5 rises 5 for every 1 right. m = 0.2 rises just 0.2 per 1 right (nearly flat).
