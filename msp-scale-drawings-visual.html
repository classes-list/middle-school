📏 Scale Drawings — Length vs. Area

Multiply length by k. Area gets multiplied by k². See it happen.

The key insight: If a scale factor is k, then every LENGTH is multiplied by k, but every AREA is multiplied by k². Volume would be k³.

🎯 Try It: Scale The Rectangle

Original (blue)

4 × 3, area = 12

Scaled (green)

8 × 6, area = 48

🧠 Big Ideas

Length scales by k. If k=2, every side is doubled.
Area scales by k². If k=2, area is 4× bigger. If k=3, area is 9× bigger. If k=½, area is ¼.
Volume scales by k³. Doubling all dimensions of a box gives 8× the volume. This is why elephants can't have mouse-shaped legs — grow all dimensions the same, weight grows FASTER than leg strength.
