📐 Language & Grammar

Similes, Metaphors, and Personification

Simile: compares two things with like or as. → The ocean is loud as a drum.
Metaphor: calls one thing another (no like/as). → The ocean is a drum.
Personification: gives a non-human thing a human action. → The wind whispered.

Try It:
  1. The stars shine _____ diamonds. (simile)
  2. My teacher is a warm sun. (this is a __________.)
  3. The old floor _____ under my feet. (personification: complete with a human action)
  4. The clouds are _____ pillows. (simile using 'like' or 'as')
