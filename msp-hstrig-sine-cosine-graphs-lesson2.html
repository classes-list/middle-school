📘 How It Works

Key idea: |a|, 2π/|b|, d.

y = a sin(bx) + d: amplitude |a|, period 2π/|b| (or 360°/|b|), midline y = d.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
For y = 0.5·cos(4(x − 1)) − 3, find the minimum value.
Answer: -3.5
✏️ Worked Example 2
For y = 4·cos(0.5x) − 2, find the amplitude.
Answer: 4
