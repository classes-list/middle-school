1️⃣ Meaning of a Log

log_b x = y means bʸ = x.

Practice:

  1. Evaluate log base 2 of 4.
  2. The equation 3^3 = 27 written in log form is log base 3 of 27 = ?
  3. Evaluate log base 4 of 1/4.
  4. The equation 10^3 = 1000 written in log form is log base 10 of 1000 = ?

2️⃣ Log Properties

Product → sum; power → multiply.

Practice:

  2. Use the power property: log base 10 of (10000)^3 = ?
  3. Use the power property: log base 3 of (3)^5 = ?
  4. Use the power property: log base 2 of (8)^5 = ?
