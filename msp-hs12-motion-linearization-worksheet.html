1️⃣ Velocity & Acceleration

Differentiate position once, then twice.

Practice:

  1. A particle’s position is s(t) = 4t³ + 3t² − 2t − 1. Find its velocity at t = 0.
  2. A particle’s position is s(t) = −t³ + t² + 4t + 4. Find its velocity at t = 3.
  3. A particle’s position is s(t) = −2t³ + t² + 3t. Find its acceleration at t = 2.
  4. A particle’s position is s(t) = −2t³ − 5t² − 5t + 1. Find its acceleration at t = 0.
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) -2 (2) -17 (3) -22 (4) -10

3️⃣ Direction of Motion

Sign of v(t).

Practice:

  1. s(t) = 2t² + t. Is the particle moving right (positive) or left (negative) at t = 1? (right or left)
  2. s(t) = 3t² + 3t. Is the particle moving right (positive) or left (negative) at t = 4? (right or left)
  3. s(t) = 2t² + 3t. Is the particle moving right (positive) or left (negative) at t = 0? (right or left)
  4. s(t) = 3t² + 2t. Is the particle moving right (positive) or left (negative) at t = 3? (right or left)
