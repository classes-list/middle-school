📘 How It Works

Key idea: Set up “height > value,” then solve.

Translate the words into an inequality: “higher than 48 ft” → h(t) > 48. Move everything to one side, factor, and find the interval. The length of time is the difference of the two endpoints.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
A ball’s height is h(t) = −16t² + 64t. For how many seconds is the ball higher than 48 feet?
Answer: 2
✏️ Worked Example 2
A ball’s height is h(t) = −16t² + 144t. For how many seconds is the ball higher than 288 feet?
Answer: 3
