1️⃣ Species Ratios

If a pond has 8 fish, 6 frogs, 3 turtles → ratio 8:6:3.

Practice:

  1. Pond: 10 fish, 5 frogs. Ratio fish:frogs?
  2. Simplify 20:15:5
  3. Class: 20 boys, 25 girls. Simplify.
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 2:1 (2) 4:3:1 (3) 4:5

2️⃣ Species Diversity

Diverse = many different species. Ratio of species to total individuals matters.

Practice:

  1. 100 animals total, 20 species. Species per animal?
  2. Pond A: 10 species, 50 total. Pond B: 3 species, 50 total. More diverse?
  3. % of population that is one species: 40 out of 200 = ?
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 1/5 or 0.2 (2) Pond A (more variety) (3) 20%

3️⃣ Simpson's Diversity (Simplified)

Higher = more diverse.

Practice:

  1. Pond with only 1 species: diverse?
  2. Pond with 10 equally-abundant species: more or less diverse?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) Not diverse at all (2) More diverse

4️⃣ Real-World Applications

Conservation biologists measure biodiversity to protect ecosystems.

Practice:

  1. Forest A: 30% oak, 30% pine, 40% other. Forest B: 90% oak, 10% pine. More diverse?
  2. Reef with 200 species vs. reef with 20. More diverse?
  3. Impact of losing 1 species out of 200 vs. 1 out of 2?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) Forest A (2) 200-species reef (3) Losing 1/2 is 50%, much bigger impact than 1/200 (0.5%)
