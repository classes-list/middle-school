📓 Math 12 — Calculus Syllabus

The Mathematics of Change · Independent accelerated study · Dr. Peter Park · Mary, Star of the Sea School

📘 Course Description

Math 12 is a first-year Calculus course modeled on the AP Calculus AB framework (limits, derivatives, applications of derivatives, integrals, applications of integrals, and differential equations), written for accelerated students working independently with step-by-step explanations.

🧭 How This Course Works

  • Read each unit page slowly — every idea is explained step by step with worked examples.
  • Open the Visual to play with the idea.
  • Do the Worksheet (answers check instantly).
  • Take the Quiz, then the Unit Test (practice first, then graded).
  • Each quarter has a Mid-Quarter and End-of-Quarter test.

📊 Grading

  • Worksheets & practice — 20%
  • Quizzes — 20%
  • Unit tests — 30%
  • Quarter tests — 30%
