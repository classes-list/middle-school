📐 Language & Grammar

Sentence Variety in Presentations

When you present, mix up sentence types so your voice does not sound flat:

• A statement tells: I wrote a story about my family.
• A question engages: Have you ever felt shy at a new school?
• An exclamation shows feeling: I was so proud of that essay!
• A command invites: Look at this photo of my family.

Try It:
  1. Write one statement about your best writing from this year.
  2. Write one question you might ask the audience.
  3. Write one exclamation showing pride.
  4. Write one command that invites the audience to look or listen.

✏️ Writing

Write a reflection: How have you grown as a reader and writer this year?

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Draw a big star. On each of the 5 points, write one thing you learned this year.

Grade 6

Write a 5-sentence reflection: your favorite unit + why + one thing you did well + one thing you want to do next year + a thank-you.

Grade 7-8

Write a full reflection essay (2-3 paragraphs). Include a specific example from each quarter and a goal for next year.
