⚖ Equations — Keep Both Sides Balanced

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. That's the whole rule.

Think of an equation as a balance scale. The = sign says "these two sides weigh the same." To solve, undo the operation using its opposite — but always do it on BOTH sides so the scale stays level.

🎯 Try It: Solve x + b = c or ax = c

x + 5 = 12

🧠 The Golden Rule

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. Subtract 5 from the left? Subtract 5 from the right. Divide by 3 on the left? Divide by 3 on the right. That keeps the scale balanced.
Undo with the opposite: + undoes −, × undoes ÷, and vice versa. To isolate x, do the OPPOSITE operation of whatever's next to it.
