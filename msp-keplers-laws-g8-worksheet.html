1️⃣ Kepler's Third Law

T² = k · a³ where T = orbital period (years), a = semi-major axis (AU). For solar system, k = 1.

Practice:

  1. Earth: a = 1 AU. T = ?
  2. If T = 8 years, a = ?
  3. If a = 4 AU, T = ?
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 1 yr (2) a³ = 64 → a = 4 AU (3) T² = 64, T = 8 yr

2️⃣ Comparing Planets

Farther = longer year. Use T² = a³ with a in AU.

Practice:

  1. Mars a = 1.52 AU. T = ?
  2. Jupiter a = 5.2 AU. T ≈ ?
  3. Neptune a ≈ 30 AU. T ≈ ?
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) T² = 3.51, T ≈ 1.87 yr (2) T² = 140.6, T ≈ 11.86 yr (3) T² = 27000, T ≈ 164 yr

3️⃣ Circular Orbit Speed

v = 2πr/T. Distance = circumference, time = one period.

4️⃣ Kepler's Other Laws

1st: orbits are ellipses. 2nd: equal areas in equal times (planets speed up near sun).

Practice:

  1. What shape is a planet's orbit?
  2. Does a comet move faster near the sun or far away?
  3. At perihelion (closest), a planet is ___ (fast/slow)?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) Ellipse (2) Near the sun (2nd law) (3) Fast
