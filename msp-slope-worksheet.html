1️⃣ What Is Slope?

SLOPE is the STEEPNESS of a line. It tells us how much y changes for every 1 unit that x changes.
Slope = RISE / RUN. Rise = vertical change. Run = horizontal change.

2️⃣ Slope Formula from Two Points

Given two points (x₁, y₁) and (x₂, y₂), the slope is:
slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) — this is "rise over run" written algebraically.
