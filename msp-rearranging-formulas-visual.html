💡 Why the triangle works: When two things multiply to give a third (like d = v × t), the top of the triangle is the product. To recover any factor, divide the product by the other factor. Covering the variable you want is a way of literally SEEING that division — the remaining two are stacked as top ÷ bottom.

🔬 Why Scientists Rearrange Formulas

Science gives you formulas in one form, but the question you're solving usually needs a different form. Density is defined as D = m ÷ V, but a lab might give you a mass and a density and ask for volume — so you rearrange to V = m ÷ D. Same formula, different variable isolated.

  • Density → mass of a mineral sample: m = D × V
  • Speed → travel time for a road trip: t = d ÷ v
  • Ohm's law → resistance from a voltage and current reading: R = V ÷ I
  • Newton's 2nd → acceleration from a known force and mass: a = F ÷ m

The triangle is a training-wheels version of solving one-step equations. Once you can do D = m/V → V = m/D in your head, you'll never draw the triangle again — but until then, it's a rock-solid safety net.
