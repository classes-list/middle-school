📍 Coordinate Plane — Click, Plot, Discover

Click anywhere on the grid to plot a point. See which quadrant it lands in.

The rule: A point is written (x, y). The x tells you how far LEFT (negative) or RIGHT (positive). The y tells you how far DOWN (negative) or UP (positive). Along the hallway, then up the stairs.

🧠 Big Ideas

Origin: (0, 0) — where the axes cross. Belongs to no quadrant.
On the x-axis means y = 0. On the y-axis means x = 0. Points on the axes aren't in any quadrant.
Always read x FIRST, then y. The point (3, 4) is DIFFERENT from (4, 3). Order matters!
