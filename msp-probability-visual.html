🎯 Probability — Spin & Compare

Theoretical probability tells you what SHOULD happen. Experimental tells you what DID happen.

The formula: P(event) = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes. On a 4-color spinner with equal sections, P(red) = 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%.

🧠 Big Ideas

The more spins, the closer to theoretical. Try 10 spins — the results might be lopsided. Try 100 — much closer to 25% each. This is called the Law of Large Numbers.
Probability is ALWAYS between 0 and 1. P = 0 means impossible. P = 1 means certain. P = 0.5 means 50/50.
