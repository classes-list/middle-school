1️⃣ What Is Density?

Density tells you how much matter (mass) is packed into a certain amount of space (volume).
Formula: D = m ÷ V. Common units: g/mL, g/cm³, kg/L, kg/m³. (1 mL = 1 cm³)

2️⃣ Rearranging the Formula

The density triangle: cover the unknown to see the formula. D = m/V, m = D × V, V = m/D.
Given any two, find the third. Solve for MASS: multiply density × volume. Solve for VOLUME: divide mass ÷ density.

3️⃣ Sink or Float?

Objects LESS dense than water (1.0 g/mL) FLOAT. Objects MORE dense than water SINK.

Practice:

  1. An object has density 0.8 g/cm³. Sink or float in water?
  2. An object has density 1.5 g/cm³. Sink or float?
  3. Ice floats in water. Why? (compare densities)

4️⃣ Mineral Identification

Geologists use density to identify minerals. You can't tell two minerals apart by looking, but their densities are unique.

5️⃣ Percent Error in a Density Lab

Real experiments never give perfect answers. Percent error tells us how close we got to the accepted value.
%Error = |Measured − Actual| ÷ Actual × 100
