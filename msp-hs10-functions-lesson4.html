📘 How It Works

Key idea: Inside moves left/right (opposite sign); outside moves up/down.

Changes inside the function move the graph sideways and work backwards: f(x − 3) moves RIGHT 3, f(x + 3) moves LEFT 3. Changes outside move it up or down the way you expect: f(x) + 3 moves UP 3.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
The graph of y = f(x) − 2 is the graph of y = f(x) shifted how many units down?
Answer: 2
✏️ Worked Example 2
