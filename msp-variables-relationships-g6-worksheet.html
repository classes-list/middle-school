1️⃣ Independent vs. Dependent

INDEPENDENT variable = the one you change or control. DEPENDENT variable = the response, "depends on" the independent.

Practice:

  1. In d = 50t (distance depends on time), which is IV? DV? IV:   DV:
  2. In # cookies eaten vs. calories consumed: IV? DV? IV:   DV:
  3. In hours studied vs. test score: IV? DV? IV:   DV:
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) IV = t, DV = d (2) cookies = IV, calories = DV (3) hours = IV, score = DV

2️⃣ Tables

Fill in from a rule.

Practice:

  1. Rule y = 3x. Fill in y for x = 1, 2, 5.
    xy = 3x
    1
    2
    5
  2. Rule y = x + 4. Fill in y for x = 0, 2, 5.
    xy = x + 4
    0
    2
    5
  3. Rule y = 2x + 1. Fill in y for x = 0, 1, 3.
    xy = 2x + 1
    0
    1
    3
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) y = 3, 6, 15 (2) y = 4, 6, 9 (3) y = 1, 3, 7

3️⃣ Graphs

Plot ordered pairs from the table.

Click where each point goes on the grid (click a point again to remove it). The points connect to show the line. Then write the rule, like y = 3x.

Practice:

  1. Plot (0,1), (1,3), (2,5) on the graph.
    What is the rule?
  2. Plot (0,0), (1,2), (2,4) on the graph.
    What is the rule?
  3. A car goes 60 mph. Plot (1, 60), (2, 120), (3, 180): x = hours, y = miles.
    What is the rule?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) rule: y = 2x + 1
(2) rule: y = 2x
(3) rule: y = 60x

4️⃣ Real Applications

Speed × time, cost × items, rate × time.

Practice:

  1. y = cost, x = items at $3 each. Rule?
  2. d = distance, t = time at 40 mph. Rule?
  3. w = weight of n cans each 5 lb. Rule?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) y = 3x (2) d = 40t (3) w = 5n
