📚 Reading Passage

Sea Turtles of Hawaiʻi
The green sea turtle, or honu, is one of the most beloved animals in Hawaiʻi. Honu can weigh over 300 pounds and live longer than 80 years. Where They Live. Honu are found in the warm ocean waters around all the Hawaiian Islands. They rest on beaches and eat seaweed and sea grass along the coast. Why They Matter. Honu are important in Hawaiian culture as a symbol of good luck and long life. They also help keep the ocean healthy by eating extra algae. How We Can Help. Green sea turtles are a protected species in Hawaiʻi. That means it is against the law to touch them or get too close. Stay at least 10 feet away when you see a honu on the beach.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

How long can green sea turtles live?FactsLonger than 80 years.

Why is it important to stay 10 feet away from a honu on the beach?ConclusionBecause they are protected by law and touching them or getting too close can harm them.

📐 Language & Grammar

Present-Tense Factual Voice + Transitions

Informational writing uses the present tense to state facts.
Turtles live in warm water. They eat sea grass.

Use transition words to move from one idea to the next: first, next, also, in addition, however, finally.

Try It:
  1. Green sea turtles _____ (live) in the ocean.
  2. They _____ (eat) sea grass and algae.
  3. _____, honu are protected by law.
  4. _____, we should stay 10 feet away from them on the beach.

✏️ Writing

Write a short informational report about your favorite animal.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Choose an animal. Draw and label a picture. Write 3 facts you know about it.

Grade 6

Write a 5-paragraph mini-report: Introduction, Where It Lives, What It Eats, Why It Matters. Include a diagram or picture.

Grade 7-8

Write a full short report (intro, 2-3 body paragraphs, closing). Include at least 2 sources listed at the bottom.
