⚪ Circles — Radius, Circumference, Area

Change the radius. Watch the diameter, circumference, and area update live.

The three formulas:
Diameter: d = 2r (twice the radius)
Circumference: C = 2πr (or πd) — how far around the outside
Area: A = πr² — how much space inside

🎯 Try It: Slide The Radius

d = 2r = 2 × 5

🧠 Big Ideas

π (pi) ≈ 3.14 — the ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter. Always. Big pizza or small pizza, C ÷ d = π.
Double the radius, DOUBLE the circumference, but QUADRUPLE the area. Because area has r² in it — the r gets squared. Try it: r=3 vs r=6 above.
Sanity check: Area is always in square units (r²). Circumference is always in plain units (r). If you get 78 for the "circumference," you probably confused it with area — check your formula.
