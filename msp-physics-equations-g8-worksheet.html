1️⃣ Speed, Distance, Time

v = d/t. Rearrange: d = vt, t = d/v.

Practice:

  1. A car goes 240 mi in 4 hr. Speed?
  2. A runner covers 100 m in 20 s. Speed?
  3. At 50 mph, how far in 3 hr?
  4. At 60 mph, how long to travel 300 mi?

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 60 mph (2) 5 m/s (3) 150 mi (4) 5 hr
