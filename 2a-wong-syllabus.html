👋 Welcome to 2A!

Welcome to Second Grade! My name is Ms. Wong and I am very excited to be your teacher this year. I have been in education since 2016 and have taught 1st, 2nd, and 5th grade in my career thus far. I enjoy working with children and helping them shine.

When I am not teaching, I am chauffeuring my kids to their basketball practices and games. I enjoy watching them play basketball. We also scrimmage together as a family — basketball is definitely a family sport for us. We also enjoy soccer. Another hobby I enjoy is crocheting — I was self-taught and taught my daughter how to crochet, too!

2nd grade is a big year! It is the year where our students can take sacramental preparation classes. It is a big commitment and we are excited to see our students soar with Jesus.

In the classroom, I have very high expectations and achievable goals for 2A. I will do my best to teach and model everything we do. I am a believer in treating others with kindness and respect. In class, we show respect by raising our hands to speak and asking permission before doing. To show kindness is to offer words of encouragement like “great job!”, “that’s a beautiful drawing!”, “you can do it!” My goal is to build positive relationships between students.

In second grade, independence is a big theme. Students will have morning tasks — putting away their own backpack, taking out their homework, and starting their morning work. We have a daily routine and we stick to it. By September, they can do it on their own without any reminders. Students take care of their own desks, cubbies, homework folder, and agendas.

I am excited for the new school year! We will have a great year soaring with our God-given talents together!

📊 Grading

Grading will be based on a variety of daily classwork, projects, assessments, and homework. Late work will be accepted, but may affect the student’s grade. In Primary we use 1–4 as our grading system.

1
Emerging
  • Beginning to understand the concept or skill
  • Requires significant support and guidance
  • Limited understanding or mastery
2
Developing
  • Demonstrates partial understanding
  • Needs occasional assistance and support
  • Progressing with notable gaps
3
Proficient
  • Meets grade-level expectations
  • Demonstrates solid understanding & application
  • Works independently with minimal errors
4
Mastery
  • Exceeds grade-level expectations
  • Thorough understanding, applies to new situations
  • Consistently accurate, higher-level thinking

Note: Report card grades are not progressive. Students receive a 1, 2, 3, or 4 based on the topics covered in that specific quarter. Grading is based on end-of-unit assessments and in-class work.

📝 Homework

Homework will typically be given Monday through Thursday. Some projects may extend through the weekend. Students will write their homework in their agenda daily.

  • Please initial that you have seen the agenda and check homework Mon.–Thurs.
  • The following day I will place a stamp, sticker, or star to indicate homework was seen and checked by parents.
  • Homework should not take more than 20 minutes a day, without reading time.
  • Reading on its own is another 15–20 minutes a day.
  • If it’s hard to complete homework, don’t hesitate to let me know in advance — I am very accommodating.

Your child will be celebrated on their birthday — they get a special shout-out on the morning announcements, a special job for the day, a birthday sticker to wear on their shirt, and 2A will sing happy birthday to them.

  • Parents must notify the teacher a week in advance if there are plans to bring pre-packaged birthday celebration snacks or goody bags.
  • The pre-packaged snacks / goody bags must be brought to the office. The office will deliver them to the classroom.

📚 Subjects & Content

✝️ Religion

Book: Blest Are We

The Sacraments — emphasis on Baptism, Reconciliation, Confirmation, and Communion. Additional topics: Vocations, Saints, and holidays.

🔤 Spelling / Phonics

Program: From Sounds to Spelling

Encompasses phonics and phonological awareness — sound-based rather than memorization. We practice weekly Target Words and High Frequency Words throughout the week to prepare for a dictation test.

📖 Reading

Programs: Epic, Freckle, book novels

Storybooks, chapter books, biographies, poetry, fables, historical text, non-fiction, realistic fiction, mysteries, fantasies, magazine articles, procedure texts, plays, informational texts, opinion pieces, and folktales.

✍️ Literacy

Books: Reader’s & Writer’s Journal, From Sounds to Spelling, Zaner-Bloser Handwriting

Writing, Grammar, Spelling, Speech, and Listening.

🧮 Math

Book: enVision Math 2.0 Volume 1 & 2

Computation, place value, time, money, estimating and graphing, problem solving, geometry, measurement, fractions, temperature, and multiplication.

🔬 Science

Books: Into Science, Mystery Science

Being a Scientist, All about Plants, New Technology, Matter, Energy, Light, Sound, and Magnets, Earth and its Resources, The Solar System, and Habitats.

🌍 Social Studies

Book: My World Interactive

Self — All about me: Grandparents Day, Community and Community Helpers, Economics, American Culture / Government, Cultural Diversity, Earth and Environment Awareness, Family History, Geography, Market Day.

Major events for 2nd graders: Grandparents Day (celebrating grandparents on campus with light refreshments) and Market Day (students write about a business they’d like to have, plan how to run it, and use math skills counting money to run a successful and profitable business).
