Who I Am & What I Bring

WIDA 2-3
Can-Do: I can introduce myself, describe my family and culture, and use complete sentences to answer questions about myself.

🎯 What You'll Learn

  • Introduce yourself in complete sentences
  • Describe family, hometown, and culture
  • Write a 5-10 sentence personal narrative
  • Use short vowels & consonant blends

📚 Reading Passage

Meet Amaya
My name is Amaya. I am eleven years old. I was born in the Marshall Islands, and my family moved to Hawaiʻi three years ago. I live with my mom, my dad, my little brother, and my grandma. At home we speak Marshallese, English, and a little Hawaiian. My favorite food is our family's kalo pie. We make it every Sunday. My grandma taught my mom, and someday my mom will teach me. At school I love art, and I want to be an artist when I grow up. My teachers say I ask a lot of questions. I am proud of that.

📐 Language & Grammar

Complete Sentences

A complete sentence has two parts: a subject (who or what) and a predicate (what they do or are). It starts with a capital letter and ends with . ? or !

Not a sentence:  plays soccer   (no subject)
Complete sentence:  Amaya plays soccer.

Try It:
  1. My brother _____ tall.
  2. I _____ from Hawaiʻi.
  3. We _____ Marshallese at home.

✏️ Writing

Write your own "Meet [Your Name]" paragraph, following Amaya's model.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Draw a picture of yourself. Write 3 sentences: name, age, and one favorite thing.

Grade 6

Write a 5-sentence paragraph: name, age, family, home language, and one favorite thing.

Grade 7-8

Write a full one-paragraph personal narrative (7-10 sentences). Include a strong opening sentence and one thing that makes you proud.
