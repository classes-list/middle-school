💰 Save for the Dream Purchase

Pick something you want. Use y = mx + b to plan when you can afford it.

🎯 The Situation

You want to buy something — sneakers, a game, a phone, a trip. You already have some money saved, and you can save more each week.

Question: How many weeks until you can afford it?

The math: Your savings grow linearly. If S = savings and w = weeks:   S = (per-week amount) × w + (current savings).
That's y = mx + b where m is your weekly savings and b is what you have now!

🎓 What You Learned

Every savings plan is a linear equation. The y-intercept (b) is what you START with. The slope (m) is how fast you grow each period. The target line is what you want to hit.

To solve "when will I hit my goal?", you set the equation equal to the target amount and solve for x. The bigger m or b, the sooner you get there. That's the whole shape of savings, loans, growth, and countdowns — all in one equation.
