% Percents — See The Piece of 100

A percent is a fraction of 100. Set it, see it, compute it.

The word says it: "Per cent" = "per hundred". 25% means 25 out of 100. Every percent is just a fraction with denominator 100.

Pie Chart

25% shaded
As a fraction: 25/100 = 1/4
As a decimal: 0.25
The math: 0.25 × 80

🧠 Big Ideas

To find a percent of a number: Multiply by the decimal. 25% of 80 = 0.25 × 80 = 20.
Quick mental math: 10% = move the decimal one place left (10% of 80 = 8). 1% = move it two places. 50% = half. 25% = one-fourth.
Tip trick: For a 15% tip on $40, do 10% ($4) plus half of that ($2) = $6. For 20%, do 10% doubled ($8).
