2️⃣ Vertex Form

y = a(x − h)² + k → vertex (h, k).

Practice:

  1. y = 2(x − 5)² + 2. What is the x-coordinate of the vertex?
  2. y = 3(x − 4)² − 5. What is the x-coordinate of the vertex?
  3. y = 2(x + 2)². What is the y-coordinate of the vertex?
  4. y = -0.5(x − 5)² − 6. What is the y-coordinate of the vertex?

3️⃣ Zeros

Set each factor equal to zero.

Practice:

  1. Find the larger zero of y = (x − 2)(x + 7).
  2. Find the smaller zero of y = x² + x − 20 (hint: factor).
  3. Find the larger zero of y = −(x − 0)(x − 3).
  4. Find the smaller zero of y = x² + x − 6 (hint: factor).

4️⃣ Max, Min & Intercepts

a < 0 → maximum; a > 0 → minimum.

Practice:

  3. Find the maximum value of y = −3x² − 12x − 20.
  4. What is the y-intercept of y = −3x² + 3x + 3?
