1️⃣ Displacement

∫v dt — backward motion subtracts.

Practice:

  1. A particle moves with v(t) = t − 4 on [0, 6]. Find its displacement.
  2. A particle moves with v(t) = 2t − 8 on [0, 6]. Find its displacement.
  3. A particle moves with v(t) = t − 2 on [0, 8]. Find its displacement.
  4. A particle moves with v(t) = 3t − 9 on [0, 9]. Find its displacement.

4️⃣ Accumulation

Start + ∫(rate in − rate out).

Practice:

  2. A tank holds 50 liters. Water flows in at 7 L/min and drains out at 2t L/min. How many liters are in the tank at t = 3 min?
  3. A tank holds 25 liters. Water flows in at 8 L/min and drains out at t L/min. How many liters are in the tank at t = 2 min?
  4. A tank holds 112 liters. Water flows in at 12 L/min and drains out at t L/min. How many liters are in the tank at t = 5 min?
