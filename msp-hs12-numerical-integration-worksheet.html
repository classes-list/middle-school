4️⃣ Over or Under?

Increasing/decreasing → left/right. Concavity → trapezoid/midpoint.

Practice:

  1. f is increasing on [a, b]. Is the left approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
  2. f is concave up on [a, b]. Is the midpoint approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
  3. f is decreasing on [a, b]. Is the left approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
  4. f is concave down on [a, b]. Is the midpoint approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) underestimate (2) underestimate (3) overestimate (4) overestimate
