1️⃣ Basic Rearranging

Apply inverse operations, keeping symbols on the other side.

Practice:

  1. A = lw. Solve for l.
  2. d = rt. Solve for t.
  3. F = ma. Solve for a.
  4. V = IR. Solve for R.
  5. E = mc². Solve for m.
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) l = A/w (2) t = d/r (3) a = F/m (4) R = V/I (5) m = E/c²

2️⃣ Multi-Step Formulas

Undo operations in reverse order.

Practice:

  1. y = mx + b. Solve for x.
  2. F = 9/5 C + 32. Solve for C.
  3. v = u + at. Solve for a.
  4. P = 2l + 2w. Solve for w.
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) x = (y − b)/m (2) C = 5/9(F − 32) (3) a = (v − u)/t (4) w = (P − 2l)/2

3️⃣ Squared/Rooted Formulas

Undo squares with square roots, and vice versa.

Practice:

  1. A = πr². Solve for r.
  2. c² = a² + b². Solve for c.
  3. V = s³. Solve for s.
  4. KE = ½mv². Solve for v.
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) r = √(A/π) (2) c = √(a² + b²) (3) s = ∛V (4) v = √(2KE/m)

4️⃣ Physics Formulas

Common physics formulas to rearrange.

Practice:

  1. p = mv (momentum). Solve for m.
  2. W = Fd (work). Solve for d.
  3. P = W/t (power). Solve for W.
  4. V = IR (voltage). Solve for I.
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) m = p/v (2) d = W/F (3) W = Pt (4) I = V/R
