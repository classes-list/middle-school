📚 Reading Passage

A Day at Star of the Sea
The morning bell rings at eight o'clock. First, Kaimana walks to his classroom and puts his backpack on his hook. Next, the teacher writes the day's schedule on the whiteboard: reading, math, recess, science, and lunch. Then, the class stands for the pledge. After that, they open their reading books and follow along as the teacher reads aloud. Finally, when Kaimana finishes his worksheet, he raises his hand to ask a question. 'When do we have art today?' he asks. The teacher smiles and points to the schedule. Art is after lunch. Kaimana can hardly wait.

📐 Language & Grammar

Asking a Question

Questions start with a question word and end with a ?

Who asks about a person. What asks about a thing. Where asks about a place. When asks about a time. Why asks about a reason. How asks about a way.

Example: When does class start?

Try It:
  1. _____ is your teacher?
  2. _____ is your favorite subject?
  3. _____ is recess?
  4. _____ do you go home?

✏️ Writing

Write a how-to paragraph about your morning routine at school.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 4-5

Draw 4 pictures of your morning at school. Label each one: First, Next, Then, Finally.

Grade 6

Write 4 sentences describing your morning at school. Start each sentence with First, Next, Then, or Finally.

Grade 7-8

Write a full 6-8 sentence how-to paragraph explaining your morning routine. Use at least 4 sequence words.
