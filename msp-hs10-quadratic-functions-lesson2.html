📘 How It Works

Key idea: x = −b/(2a), then plug in.

For y = ax² + bx + c, the vertex’s x-coordinate is −b/(2a). Plug that x back into the equation to get the y-coordinate. This x = −b/(2a) is also the axis of symmetry.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Find the y-coordinate of the vertex of y = x² + 8x + 15.
Answer: -1
✏️ Worked Example 2
Find the y-coordinate of the vertex of y = 2x² + 12x + 12.
Answer: -6
