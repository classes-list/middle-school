📘 How It Works

Key idea: Swap x and y, then solve for y.

To find an inverse: write y = f(x), swap x and y, then solve for y. For a linear function you undo the steps in reverse order. The slope of the inverse is the reciprocal of the original slope. To find f⁻¹(k), you can also just solve f(x) = k.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = −3x − 8. Find f⁻¹(7).
Answer: -5
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = −4x + 7. The inverse is f⁻¹(x) = mx + c. What is m? (decimal ok)
Answer: -0.25
