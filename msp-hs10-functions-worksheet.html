2️⃣ Average Rate of Change

Slope between two points on the graph.

Practice:

  1. Find the average rate of change of f(x) = x² from x = -3 to x = -2.
  2. Find the average rate of change of f(x) = 3x − 1 from x = 0 to x = 1.
  3. Find the average rate of change of f(x) = x³ from x = -3 to x = -2.
  4. Find the average rate of change of f(x) = x³ from x = -2 to x = -1.

3️⃣ Shifts

Inside moves left/right (opposite sign); outside moves up/down.

Practice:

  1. The graph of y = f(x + 1) is the graph of y = f(x) shifted how many units left?
  2. What is the smallest x-value in the domain of f(x) = √(x + 1)?
  3. What is the smallest x-value in the domain of f(x) = √(x + 3)?
  4. The graph of y = f(x) − 5 is the graph of y = f(x) shifted how many units down?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 1 (2) -1 (3) -3 (4) 5

4️⃣ Vertex of a Transformed Graph

y = a·f(x − h) + k has vertex (h, k).

Practice:

  1. The graph of y = −|x + 4| is a transformed |x|. What is the x-coordinate of its vertex?
  2. The graph of y = |x − 4| + 1 is a transformed |x|. What is the y-coordinate of its vertex?
  3. The graph of y = 2|x − 2| + 5 is a transformed |x|. What is the x-coordinate of its vertex?
  4. The graph of y = −(x − 1)² + 2 is a transformed x². What is the x-coordinate of its vertex?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) -4 (2) 1 (3) 2 (4) 1
