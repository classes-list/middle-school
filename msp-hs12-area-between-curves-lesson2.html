📘 How It Works

Key idea: ∫(top − bottom) between crossings.

Find where the curves cross (set them equal). Area = ∫(top − bottom) dx between the crossing points. Check which is on top by testing a point in between.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Find the area between y = 3x and y = x² (nearest hundredth).
Answer: 4.50
✏️ Worked Example 2
