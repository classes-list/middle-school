📏 KHDUdcm Staircase

Every step on the staircase = ×10 or ÷10. Go DOWN the stairs (K → m): multiply by 10 each step. Go UP the stairs (m → K): divide by 10 each step. Equivalent to moving the decimal!

🎯 Try It: Convert Any Metric Unit

🧠 Big Ideas

Down the stairs (K→H→D→Unit→d→c→m): multiply by 10 per step (decimal moves RIGHT).
Up the stairs (m→c→d→Unit→D→H→K): divide by 10 per step (decimal moves LEFT).
Common needed conversions: 1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm, 1 L = 1000 mL, 1 kg = 1000 g.
