📘 How It Works

Key idea: Solve f′(x) = 0.

Critical points are where f′(x) = 0 or f′ is undefined. Differentiate, set equal to zero, and solve.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = x³ − 12x. Find the smaller critical point (x-value).
Answer: -2
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = x³ + 6x². Find the smaller critical point (x-value).
Answer: -4
