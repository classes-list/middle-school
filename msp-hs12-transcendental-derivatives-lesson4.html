📘 How It Works

Key idea: (f⁻¹)′(b) = 1/f′(a) where f(a) = b.

To find (f⁻¹)′(b): first find a with f(a) = b (it is often given). Then compute f′(a) and take the reciprocal: (f⁻¹)′(b) = 1/f′(a). Do not plug b into f′!

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = 3x³ + x + 2 is one-to-one, and f(−1) = −2. Find (f⁻¹)′(−2) (fraction or decimal).
Answer: 1/10
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = x³ + x + 2 is one-to-one, and f(−2) = −8. Find (f⁻¹)′(−8) (fraction or decimal).
Answer: 1/13
