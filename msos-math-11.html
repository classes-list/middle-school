📓 Math 11 — Pre-Calculus Syllabus

Getting Ready for Calculus · Independent accelerated study · Dr. Peter Park · Mary, Star of the Sea School

📘 Course Description

Math 11 is a Pre-Calculus course aligned to the Common Core State Standards (+ standards for advanced students): functions and inverses, polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions, full trigonometry, vectors, polar and complex numbers, conic sections, series, and an introduction to limits.

🧭 How This Course Works

  • Each unit has daily lessons: Lesson 1 is the unit overview; Lessons 2–5 each teach one skill with two worked examples and practice.
  • Read slowly — every idea is explained step by step.
  • Open the Visual to play with the idea.
  • Do the Worksheet (answers check instantly).
  • Take the Quiz, then the Unit Test (practice first, then graded).
  • Each quarter has a Mid-Quarter and End-of-Quarter test.

📊 Grading

  • Worksheets & practice — 20%
  • Quizzes — 20%
  • Unit tests — 30%
  • Quarter tests — 30%
