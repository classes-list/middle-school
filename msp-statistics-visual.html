🧠 Big Ideas

Outliers pull the MEAN. One giant value can drag the mean up (or down) far from where most data sits. The MEDIAN is safer for skewed data.
Mode tells you what's most common — great for shoe sizes, favorite colors, most-repeated grade.
Range tells you how spread out the data is. Small range = tightly clustered. Big range = data all over the place.
