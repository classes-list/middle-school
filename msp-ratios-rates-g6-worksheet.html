1️⃣ Ratio Notation

A ratio compares two quantities. Three ways to write 3 to 5: 3:5, 3/5, 3 to 5.

  1. A class has 10 boys and 14 girls. Boys : girls =
  2. A bag has 8 red and 12 blue marbles. Red : total =
  3. 3 cups flour to 2 cups sugar. Flour : sugar =

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 10:14 = 5:7 (2) 8:20 = 2:5 (3) 3:2

2️⃣ Equivalent Ratios & Simplifying

Multiply or divide both parts by the same number to get equivalent ratios.

Practice:

  1. Simplify 15:25
  2. Simplify 24:36
  3. 2:5 = ? : 20
  4. 3:4 = 12:?

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 3:5 (2) 2:3 (3) 8 (4) 16

5️⃣ Best Buy Problems

Compare unit rates. Smaller price per unit = better deal.

Practice:

  1. 10 oz for $2.50 vs 15 oz for $3.30. Better?
  2. 3 lb for $6 vs 5 lb for $9. Better?

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) 10 oz: $0.25/oz; 15 oz: $0.22/oz → 15 oz better (2) 3 lb: $2/lb; 5 lb: $1.80/lb → 5 lb better
