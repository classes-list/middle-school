📘 How It Works

Key idea: Increasing/decreasing → left/right. Concavity → trapezoid/midpoint.

Increasing function: left rectangles sit under the curve (under), right ones poke above (over). Decreasing: the reverse. Concave up: trapezoid tops lie above the curve (over), midpoint is under. Concave down: the reverse.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f is increasing on [a, b]. Is the right approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
Answer: overestimate
✏️ Worked Example 2
f is concave down on [a, b]. Is the trapezoid approximation of ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx an overestimate or an underestimate?
Answer: underestimate
