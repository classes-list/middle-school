🔗 Partial Sums of a Geometric Series

Add the same amount, or multiply by the same amount.

Big idea: Each bar is a partial sum. When |r| < 1, the bars level off at a₁/(1 − r); when |r| ≥ 1 they run away.

🧠 What To Notice
