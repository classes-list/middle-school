2️⃣ What Makes a Relationship Proportional?

Two quantities are PROPORTIONAL if their ratio is always the same — the y ÷ x ratio is constant.
Test: Compute y ÷ x for each row. If ALL ratios are the same → proportional. If they differ → NOT proportional.

3️⃣ Constant of Proportionality (k) and y = kx

If two quantities are proportional, the equation is y = kx, where k = y ÷ x is the constant of proportionality (or unit rate).
Steps: (1) Find k = y ÷ x from any row. (2) Write y = kx. (3) Use to find missing values.

Practice:

  1. Table: (x=3, y=15), (x=5, y=25). Find k = and write the equation y =
  2. A car goes 60 miles in 1 hour. Find k. Distance for 4 hours?
  3. 4 apples cost $6. Find k. Cost of 10 apples?
  4. A recipe: 2 cups flour makes 12 cookies. Find k. Cups for 30 cookies?
  5. y = 8x. What is k? What is y when x = 7?

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) k = 5, y = 5x | (2) k = 60 mi/hr, distance for 4 hrs = 240 mi | (3) k = $1.50/apple, cost of 10 = $15 | (4) k = 6 cookies/cup → cups = 30/6 = 5 cups | (5) k = 8, y = 56

4️⃣ Graphs of Proportional Relationships

Proportional relationships graph as STRAIGHT LINES through the ORIGIN (0, 0). The slope equals k.
3 tests for a proportional graph: (1) It's a straight line. (2) It passes through (0, 0). (3) Its slope = k.

Practice:

  1. A line passes through (0,0) and (4, 12). Is it proportional? What is k?
  2. A line passes through (0, 2) and (3, 8). Is it proportional?
  3. Graph of y = 4x — where does it cross the y-axis?
  4. Two points: (2, 10) and (5, 25). Proportional? Find k.
  5. At (1, 3), what does the y-value mean?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) Yes (through origin), k = 3 | (2) No (does not pass through origin) | (3) At y = 0 (the origin) | (4) Yes — 10/2 = 5 = 25/5, k = 5 | (5) It's the unit rate: when x = 1, y = k

5️⃣ Real-World Applications

Proportional relationships are everywhere — pricing, scaling, speed, mixing.

Practice:

  1. 3 lbs of grapes cost $4.50. Find k. Cost of 8 lbs?
  2. A printer prints 15 pages in 2 minutes. Find k. Pages in 10 minutes?
  3. Density is proportional: 5 mL of oil has mass 4 g. Density (k) = ? Mass of 20 mL?
  4. A recipe scaling: 3 eggs make 12 pancakes. Eggs for 40 pancakes?
—

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) k = $1.50/lb, cost of 8 lbs = $12 | (2) k = 7.5 pages/min, in 10 min = 75 pages | (3) k = 0.8 g/mL, mass of 20 mL = 16 g | (4) k = 4 pancakes/egg → eggs = 40/4 = 10 eggs
