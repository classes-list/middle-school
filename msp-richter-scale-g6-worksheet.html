1️⃣ The Richter Scale

Each 1-point increase = 10× the AMPLITUDE (ground motion) and ~32× the ENERGY.
Amplitude comparison: Magnitude difference n means 10ⁿ times greater amplitude.

Practice:

  1. Earthquake magnitude 7 vs 5. How many × the amplitude?
  2. Magnitude 6 vs 4?
  3. Magnitude 8 vs 5?
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 10² = 100× (2) 10² = 100× (3) 10³ = 1000×

3️⃣ Comparing Real Earthquakes

1906 San Francisco: 7.9. 2011 Japan: 9.0. Kobe 1995: 6.9. Chile 1960: 9.5 (largest ever recorded).

Practice:

  2. How many × is 8 vs 6?
  3. Which is larger and by how much: 6.5 vs 5.5?

4️⃣ Energy on Richter

Each magnitude step = ~32× energy. Two steps ≈ 32 × 32 = ~1000× energy.

Practice:

  1. Approximate energy of 7 vs 5?
  2. Approximate energy of 8 vs 6?
  3. If magnitude 7 releases 1 unit of energy, magnitude 9 releases about how many?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) ~1000× (2) ~1000× (3) ~1000 units
