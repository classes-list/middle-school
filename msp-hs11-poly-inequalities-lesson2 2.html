📘 How It Works

Key idea: Factor, find the zeros, test between them.

Move everything to one side and factor. The zeros split the number line into intervals. A quadratic opening up is negative BETWEEN its zeros and positive OUTSIDE them. So “< 0” → between; “> 0” → outside.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Solve x² + 6x < 0. The solution is an interval from a to b. Find a.
Answer: -6
✏️ Worked Example 2
Solve x² − 8x + 15 > 0. The solution is x < a or x > b. Find a.
Answer: 3
