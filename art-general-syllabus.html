🤝 Classroom Expectations

Students are expected to come to class ready to learn and prepared to follow all classroom and school rules. They must follow safety guidelines and use classroom materials as directed. Responsible use of materials, respect for others, staying focused, perseverance, and a willingness to learn are all essential expectations.

Ample class time is provided to complete art projects, so there should be no homework assigned for art class. Please refer to the grading rubric below.

At least 50% of the lessons must be completed to receive a comprehensive grade in Art class. Each lesson covers different elements of art, so missing lessons may negatively impact overall grades.

📊 Assessments

Each art project is worth 20 points and is graded using five criteria outlined in the rubrics below, with each criterion valued at 4 points. You’ll notice the focus isn’t on being a “good” artist. Instead, the rubric emphasizes skill development, creativity, following directions, and positive classroom behavior.

Middle School late-work policy: One point is deducted for each day an assignment is late. Students are welcome to take projects home or make arrangements with me to complete them during school hours. Students should not come during lunch periods without making arrangements with me in advance.

NeatnessVery neat! PerfectMostly neatAlmost neatNot neat at all — very messy
CreativityVery creative! Original ideasSomewhat creative — some original ideasNot very creative — some borrowed ideasNo creative ideas — I copied everything
Followed directionsI followed all of the directionsI followed most of the directionsI followed “some” of the directionsI had trouble following the directions
Work habitsI work quietly and clean up after myselfI chat while working but clean up after myselfI am chatty while I work — have to be reminded to clean upI am loud / disruptive while working and I don’t clean up
I do not grade based on who is a “good” artist (as in can draw realistically). I grade according to the rubric — considering following directions, neat work, creativity, using the concepts being taught, cleaning up, and effort. A student who cannot draw well but pays attention, helps her/his teammates, TRIES their best, and cleans up after themselves can earn an A.
