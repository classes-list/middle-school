📘 How It Works

Key idea: Swap x and y, then solve for y.

To find f⁻¹: write y = f(x), swap x and y, solve for y. To find f⁻¹ at a number k, you can simply solve f(x) = k. Check: f(f⁻¹(k)) should give k back.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = −2x. Find f⁻¹(4).
Answer: -2
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = 3x + 2. Find f⁻¹(9).
Answer: 2.3333
