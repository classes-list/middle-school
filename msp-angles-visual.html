📐 Angle Pairs — Complementary, Supplementary, Vertical

Slide the angle. Watch its partner change with it.

Three key pairs:
Complementary — two angles that add to 90° (form a right angle).
Supplementary — two angles that add to 180° (form a straight line).
Vertical — two angles across from each other where lines cross. Always EQUAL.

🎯 Try It: Pick The Type, Slide The Angle

🧠 Memory Trick

Complementary → Corner (90°, a right angle)
Supplementary → Straight line (180°)
Vertical → across the X, always the same Value
