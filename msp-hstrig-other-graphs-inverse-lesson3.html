📘 How It Works

Key idea: Where the denominator is zero.

Asymptotes happen where the function divides by zero: tan and sec where cos = 0 (90°, 270°, …); cot and csc where sin = 0 (0°, 180°, …).

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
What is the smallest positive x-value (degrees) where y = csc x has a vertical asymptote?
Answer: 180
✏️ Worked Example 2
What is the smallest positive x-value (degrees) where y = tan x has a vertical asymptote?
Answer: 90
