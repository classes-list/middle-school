1️⃣ What Is a System?

A SYSTEM = two or more equations solved together. Solution = a point (x,y) that satisfies BOTH equations. Graphically, that's where the lines CROSS.

Practice:

  1. Check: is (2,5) a solution to y = 2x + 1 and y = x + 3?
  2. Check: is (1,4) a solution to y = 3x + 1 and y = 2x + 2?
  3. How many solutions do two parallel lines have?
  4. How many solutions do two identical lines have?

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) y=2(2)+1=5 ✓, y=2+3=5 ✓ → Yes (2) y=3+1=4 ✓, y=2+2=4 ✓ → Yes (3) Zero (never cross) (4) Infinite

2️⃣ Solving by Graphing

Graph both lines; the intersection is the solution.

Practice:

  1. Graph y = x + 2 and y = -x + 4. Solution?
  2. Graph y = 2x − 1 and y = -x + 5. Solution?
  3. Graph y = x and y = 3. Solution?

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) (1, 3) (2) (2, 3) (3) (3, 3)

3️⃣ Substitution Method

Solve one equation for a variable, then substitute into the other.
Steps: (1) Isolate one variable. (2) Substitute into the other equation. (3) Solve for the remaining variable. (4) Back-substitute.

Practice:

  1. Solve: y = 2x + 1 and y = x + 4
  2. Solve: y = 3x and y = x + 6
  3. Solve: y = x + 2 and 2x + y = 8

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 2x+1 = x+4 → x=3, y=7. (3,7) (2) 3x = x+6 → x=3, y=9. (3,9) (3) 2x+(x+2)=8 → x=2, y=4. (2,4)

4️⃣ Elimination Method

Add or subtract equations to eliminate a variable.

Practice:

  1. x + y = 10, x − y = 4
  2. 2x + y = 7, x − y = 2
  3. 3x + 2y = 13, x + 2y = 7

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) Add: 2x=14 → x=7, y=3. (7,3) (2) Add: 3x=9 → x=3, y=1. (3,1) (3) Subtract: 2x=6 → x=3, y=2. (3,2)

5️⃣ Word Problems

Set up two equations from the situation.

Practice:

  1. Two numbers sum to 20, difference is 4. Find them.
  2. Adult tickets $8, child tickets $5. 20 tickets sold, $130 collected. How many of each?
  3. In 5 years, Tom will be twice as old as his brother Sam. Tom is now 3 years older than Sam. Ages?

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) x+y=20, x−y=4 → x=12, y=8 (2) A+C=20, 8A+5C=130 → A=10, C=10 (3) T+5=2(S+5), T=S+3 → T=8, S=5
