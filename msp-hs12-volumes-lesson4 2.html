📘 How It Works

Key idea: π∫(R² − r²) dx.

Washer method: V = π∫(R² − r²) dx, where R is the outer radius and r is the inner radius. Square each first, then subtract.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
The region between y = x and y = x² (0 ≤ x ≤ 1) is spun around the x-axis. Find the volume (nearest thousandth).
