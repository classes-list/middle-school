📓 Math 9 — Geometry Syllabus

Proving the World with Shapes · Independent accelerated study · Dr. Peter Park · Mary, Star of the Sea School

📘 Course Description

Math 9 is a full high-school Geometry course aligned to the Common Core State Standards (G-CO, G-SRT, G-C, G-GPE, G-GMD, G-MG), written for accelerated middle-school students working independently. Every unit explains ideas step by step with worked examples, pictures, and an interactive visual, followed by a worksheet, quiz, and unit test.

🧭 How This Course Works

  • Each unit has daily lessons: Lesson 1 is the unit overview; Lessons 2–5 each teach one skill with two worked examples and practice.
  • Read slowly — every idea is explained step by step.
  • Open the Visual to play with the idea.
  • Do the Worksheet (answers check instantly).
  • Take the Quiz, then the Unit Test (practice first, then graded).
  • Each quarter has a Mid-Quarter and End-of-Quarter test.

📊 Grading

  • Worksheets & practice — 20%
  • Quizzes — 20%
  • Unit tests — 30%
  • Quarter tests — 30%
