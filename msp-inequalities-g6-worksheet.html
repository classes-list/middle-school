1️⃣ Symbols

< less than · > greater than · ≤ less than or equal · ≥ greater than or equal.

Practice:

  1. Which symbol: 'x is at least 5'?
  2. 'x is no more than 10'?
  3. Is 7 > 5?
  4. Is 3 ≤ 3?
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) ≥ (2) ≤ (3) Yes (4) Yes

2️⃣ Solving

Just like equations: isolate the variable.

Practice:

  1. x + 3 > 8
  2. x − 5 < 2
  3. 2x ≥ 10
  4. x/3 < 6
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) x > 5 (2) x < 7 (3) x ≥ 5 (4) x < 18

3️⃣ Graphing on Number Line

Open circle for </>, closed for ≤/≥. Arrow toward solution.

To graph: click the number line to place the circle, choose open or closed, then choose the arrow direction. Then click Check Answers.

Practice:

  1. Graph x > 3
  2. Graph x ≤ -2
  3. Graph x < 0
  4. Graph x ≥ 5
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) x > 3: open circle at 3, arrow right
(2) x ≤ -2: closed circle at -2, arrow left
(3) x < 0: open circle at 0, arrow left
(4) x ≥ 5: closed circle at 5, arrow right

4️⃣ Word Problems

"at least" ≥, "at most" ≤.

Practice:

  1. Ticket cost is at least $10. Write inequality.
  2. No more than 30 students per bus. Write.
  3. Grade must be at least 70. Write.
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) c ≥ 10 (2) s ≤ 30 (3) g ≥ 70
