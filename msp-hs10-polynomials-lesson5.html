📘 How It Works

Key idea: Degree (even/odd) + sign of the leading coefficient.

End behavior depends only on the leading term. Even degree: both ends go the same way (up if the leading coefficient is positive). Odd degree: the ends go opposite ways (left down, right up if positive).

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
For y = -3x^7 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far right?
Answer: down
✏️ Worked Example 2
For y = −x^5 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far left?
Answer: up
