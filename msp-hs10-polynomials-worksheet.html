1️⃣ Degree & Operations

Degree = highest power. Multiply term by term.

Practice:

  1. What is the degree of −6x^7 + 3x^3 − 5x^5?
  2. Multiply (x − 4)(1x + 3). What is the coefficient of x?
  3. What is the degree of x^6 − 8x^8 + 3x^5?
  4. Multiply (x + 2)(1x + 5). What is the constant term?

2️⃣ Remainder Theorem

Remainder of p(x) ÷ (x − a) is p(a).

Practice:

  1. Find the remainder when p(x) = 2x³ + x² + 3x − 2 is divided by (x − 2).
  2. Find the remainder when p(x) = x³ + 3x² − 2x − 3 is divided by (x − 0).
  3. Find the remainder when p(x) = −3x³ − x² − 2x + 1 is divided by (x − 0).
  4. Find the remainder when p(x) = −x³ + 2x − 3 is divided by (x − 2).
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 24 (2) -3 (3) 1 (4) -7

3️⃣ Zeros

Each factor gives a zero.

Practice:

  1. What is the largest zero of y = (x + 3)(x − 1)(x − 3)?
  2. What is the largest zero of y = (x + 3)(x − 0)(x − 2)?
  3. What is the largest zero of y = (x + 1)(x − 0)(x − 1)?
  4. What is the largest zero of y = (x + 2)(x + 1)(x − 1)?
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 3 (2) 2 (3) 1 (4) 1

4️⃣ End Behavior

Degree (even/odd) + sign of the leading coefficient.

Practice:

  1. For y = 4x^6 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far right?
  2. For y = 4x^3 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far right?
  3. For y = −x^6 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far left?
  4. For y = 2x^5 + 3x − 1, does the graph go up or down on the far right?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) up (2) up (3) down (4) up
