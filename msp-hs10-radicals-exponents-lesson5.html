📘 How It Works

Key idea: Isolate, square, and check.

Isolate the radical, then raise both sides to the power that undoes it (square a square root). Solve, then CHECK in the original equation — squaring can create extra (extraneous) answers.

👀 Watch Two Examples

Answer: 1
