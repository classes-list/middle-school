📘 How It Works

Key idea: ∫v dt — backward motion subtracts.

Displacement = ∫ₐᵇ v(t) dt. Integrate the velocity and subtract F(b) − F(a). Areas below the t-axis count as negative, so the answer can be negative (the object ended up behind where it started).

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
A particle moves with v(t) = 3t − 12 on [0, 6]. Find its displacement.
Answer: -18
✏️ Worked Example 2

Answer: 36
