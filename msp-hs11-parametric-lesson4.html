📘 How It Works

Key idea: Slope = (y-step)/(x-step); circle: center (h, k), radius r.

For a line x = x₀ + at, y = y₀ + bt, each step of t moves a right and b up, so the slope is b/a. For x = h + r cos t, y = k + r sin t, the center is (h, k) and the radius is r.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
The line x = −3 + t, y = −3 − 5t. What is its slope?
Answer: -5
✏️ Worked Example 2
x = 1 + 4 cos t, y = −4 + 4 sin t traces a circle. What is its radius?
Answer: 4
