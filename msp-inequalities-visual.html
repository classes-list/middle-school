📏 Inequalities — Shade The Solutions

Every inequality is a whole region of numbers, not just one.

The circle rules: Open circle ○ for < or > (does NOT include the number). Closed circle ● for ≤ or ≥ (INCLUDES it). Then shade the arrow toward every number that works.

🧠 Big Ideas

Solving inequalities works just like solving equations — with ONE exception: when you multiply or divide by a negative number, FLIP the inequality sign.
Test a number from the shaded region to check your work. Is it true? Then you shaded correctly.
