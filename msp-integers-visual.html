➕➖ Integer Operations — Arrows On The Number Line

Every add is an arrow. Every subtract is a flip.

The rule: Adding a positive means move RIGHT. Adding a negative means move LEFT.
Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite: 5 − 3 = 5 + (−3). Flip the sign and add.

🎯 Try It: Watch The Arrows Add Up

Two rules cover almost every case:
(1) Same signs? Add the numbers, keep the sign. −5 + −3 = −8.
(2) Different signs? Subtract the smaller from the bigger, keep the sign of the bigger. −7 + 3 = −4 (the −7 wins).
