📘 How It Works

Key idea: Undo the operations in reverse order.

Some functions (like x²) need a restricted domain (x ≥ 0) to have an inverse. To find f⁻¹(k), solve f(x) = k and keep only the answer in the allowed domain. Undo in reverse: subtract the constant, then take the root.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
f(x) = √x + 5 (x ≥ 0). Find f⁻¹(8).
Answer: 9
✏️ Worked Example 2
f(x) = x² + 1 with domain x ≥ 0. Find f⁻¹(5).
Answer: 2
