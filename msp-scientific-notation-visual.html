🔬 Scientific Notation — Big Numbers, Tiny Numbers

Every number, no matter how big or small, fits into one clean pattern.

The form: a × 10ⁿ where 1 ≤ a < 10 and n is an integer.
Positive n → BIG number (move decimal right). Negative n → TINY number (move decimal left).

🎯 Try It: Build The Notation

🧠 Big Ideas

Multiplying by 10ⁿ: if n is positive, move the decimal right n places. If n is negative, move the decimal left |n| places.
The coefficient must be between 1 and 10 (not including 10). So 12 × 10³ is NOT in scientific notation — rewrite as 1.2 × 10⁴.
Multiplying two scientific numbers: multiply the coefficients, ADD the exponents. (2 × 10³)(4 × 10⁵) = 8 × 10⁸.
