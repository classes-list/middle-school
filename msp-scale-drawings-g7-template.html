SCALE: 1 in on the original = 4 in on your paper  →  Ratio 1 : 4
Instructions
  1. Label your grids. Columns A–H across the top, rows 1–8 down the side (already labeled on both pages).
  2. Copy one square at a time. Look only at the tiny lines inside one small square. Copy those lines into the matching big square — same relative position, angles, and curves.
  3. Do NOT draw the whole picture at once. Focus on square-by-square accuracy. If every square is copied faithfully, the whole picture looks right.
  4. When finished, erase the grid lines and color your drawing.
