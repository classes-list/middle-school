🔺 Triangle Angles Always Sum to 180°

Every triangle, no matter how it's drawn — big or tiny, sharp or wide — has angles that add up to exactly 180°. Change two angles, and the third is FORCED.

🎯 Try It: Set Two Angles, See The Third

🧠 Triangle Types (by angles)

Acute triangle: all three angles < 90°.
Right triangle: exactly one 90° angle.
Obtuse triangle: one angle > 90° (the other two must be small).
Since angles sum to 180°, you can never have TWO angles ≥ 90° in the same triangle.
