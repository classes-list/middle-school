📘 How It Works

Key idea: Complete the square; x² = 4py.

For a circle, complete the square in x and in y to get (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r². For a parabola x² = 4py, the focus is p units from the vertex.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
Complete the square to find the radius of x² + y² + 10x + 12y + 36 = 0.
Answer: 5
✏️ Worked Example 2
The parabola x² = 4y has its focus at (0, p). What is p?
Answer: 1
