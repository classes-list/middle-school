1️⃣ Solving One-Step Equations

Do the INVERSE operation to both sides. + undoes −, × undoes ÷.
Goal: Isolate the variable. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

Practice:

  1. x + 5 = 12
  2. x − 3 = 8
  3. 4x = 20
  4. x / 3 = 7
  5. x + 15 = 9
  6. 5x = 45
  7. x/2 = -6
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) x = 7 | (2) x = 11 | (3) x = 5 | (4) x = 21 | (5) x = -6 | (6) x = 9 | (7) x = -12

2️⃣ Two-Step Equations

Undo addition/subtraction FIRST, then undo multiplication/division. (Reverse order of operations.)
Think: to unwrap, undo the outermost operation first.

Practice:

  1. 2x + 3 = 11
  2. 3x − 5 = 7
  3. x/2 + 4 = 10
  4. 5x + 8 = 23
  5. 4x − 7 = 9
  6. x/3 − 2 = 5
  7. 2x + 15 = 5
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) x = 4 | (2) x = 4 | (3) x = 12 | (4) x = 3 | (5) x = 4 | (6) x = 21 | (7) x = -5

3️⃣ Distributive Property

When there's a coefficient outside parentheses, distribute (multiply) it to each term inside.
a(b + c) = ab + ac. Example: 3(x + 2) = 3x + 6.

Practice:

  1. 2(x + 3) = 10
  2. 3(x − 4) = 15
  3. -2(x + 5) = 8
  4. 4(2x − 1) = 20
  5. 5(x + 2) = 3x + 20
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) 2x + 6 = 10 → x = 2 | (2) 3x - 12 = 15 → x = 9 | (3) -2x - 10 = 8 → x = -9 | (4) 8x - 4 = 20 → x = 3 | (5) 5x + 10 = 3x + 20 → 2x = 10 → x = 5

4️⃣ Variables on Both Sides

Move all variable terms to ONE side, all number terms to the OTHER side.

Practice:

  1. 3x + 5 = x + 11
  2. 5x − 2 = 3x + 8
  3. 2x + 4 = 4x − 6
  4. 6x − 3 = 2x + 9
  5. 7x + 10 = 3x − 6
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) 3x - x = 11 - 5 → 2x = 6 → x = 3 | (2) 2x = 10 → x = 5 | (3) -2x = -10 → x = 5 | (4) 4x = 12 → x = 3 | (5) 4x = -16 → x = -4

5️⃣ Special Cases: No Solution / Infinite Solutions

After solving, if you get:
• A TRUE statement (like 5 = 5) → INFINITE solutions.
• A FALSE statement (like 3 = 7) → NO solution.
• A number for x → ONE solution.

Practice:

  1. 2x + 5 = 2x + 5 → ?
  2. 3x + 4 = 3x + 9 → ?
  3. 5(x + 2) = 5x + 10 → ?
  4. 2x + 3 = 2x + 7 → ?
  5. x + x = 2x → ?
—

Answer Key — Section 5

(1) 5 = 5 → infinite solutions | (2) 4 = 9 → no solution | (3) 5x + 10 = 5x + 10 → infinite solutions | (4) 3 = 7 → no solution | (5) 2x = 2x → infinite solutions

6️⃣ Word Problems

Set up the equation from the situation, then solve.

Practice:

  1. The sum of a number and 8 is 21. Find the number.
  2. Twice a number, decreased by 5, is 17. Find the number.
  3. A rectangle has perimeter 30. Length is twice the width. Find dimensions.
  4. John has $45. He spends $8 per week. In how many weeks will he have $5 left?
—

Answer Key — Section 6

(1) x + 8 = 21 → x = 13 | (2) 2x - 5 = 17 → x = 11 | (3) 2(L + W) = 30, L = 2W → 2(2W + W) = 30 → W = 5, L = 10 | (4) 45 - 8w = 5 → 8w = 40 → w = 5 weeks
