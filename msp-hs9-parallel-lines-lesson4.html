📘 How It Works

Key idea: Parallel → same slope. Perpendicular → opposite reciprocal.

Parallel lines have the SAME slope. Perpendicular lines have slopes that are opposite reciprocals: flip the fraction and change the sign (2 → −½). Their product is −1.

👀 Watch Two Examples

✏️ Worked Example 1
A line has slope 1/2. What is the slope of a line parallel to it? (fraction or decimal)
Answer: 1/2
✏️ Worked Example 2
A line has slope 1. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it? (fraction or decimal)
Answer: -1
