1️⃣ Name the Pair & Find the Angle

Equal pairs: corresponding, alternate interior, alternate exterior. Same-side interior add to 180°.

Practice:

  1. Lines m and n are parallel. One alternate exterior angle measures 62°. Find the measure of its partner angle.
  2. Lines m and n are parallel. One corresponding angle measures 142°. Find the measure of its partner angle.
  3. Lines m and n are parallel. One alternate exterior angle measures 102°. Find the measure of its partner angle.
  4. Lines m and n are parallel. One alternate exterior angle measures 46°. Find the measure of its partner angle.
—

Answer Key — Section 1

(1) 62 (2) 142 (3) 102 (4) 46

2️⃣ Solve for x

Decide "equal" or "add to 180", then solve.

Practice:

  1. Lines m and n are parallel. The corresponding angles measure (3x + 7)° and (x + 45)°. Find x.
  2. Lines m and n are parallel. The alternate interior angles measure (4x − 10)° and (2x + 32)°. Find x.
  3. Lines m and n are parallel. The alternate exterior angles measure (3x + 19)° and (x + 75)°. Find x.
  4. Lines m and n are parallel. The alternate exterior angles measure (3x − 4)° and (2x + 19)°. Find x.
—

Answer Key — Section 2

(1) 19 (2) 21 (3) 28 (4) 23

3️⃣ Slopes

Parallel → same slope. Perpendicular → opposite reciprocal.

Practice:

  1. A line has slope -4/5. What is the slope of a line parallel to it? (fraction or decimal)
  2. A line has slope 5/4. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it? (fraction or decimal)
  3. A line has slope -1/2. What is the slope of a line parallel to it? (fraction or decimal)
  4. A line has slope -3/2. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it? (fraction or decimal)
—

Answer Key — Section 3

(1) -4/5 (2) -4/5 (3) -1/2 (4) 2/3

4️⃣ Parallel, Perpendicular, or Neither?

Compare the slopes.

Practice:

  1. Line 1: y = -2x + 2. Line 2: y = 0.5x + 0. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
  2. Line 1: y = -3x − 3. Line 2: y = -3x + 0. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
  3. Line 1: y = 3x + 1. Line 2: y = 4x − 1. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
  4. Line 1: y = -2x − 3. Line 2: y = 0.5x − 2. Are the lines parallel, perpendicular, or neither?
—

Answer Key — Section 4

(1) perpendicular (2) parallel (3) neither (4) perpendicular
