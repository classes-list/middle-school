Physical Education Syllabus

Middle School Physical Education (6th-8th Grade) · SY 2026-2027
Instructor: Mr. Lyman Lacro · Email: llacro@starofthesea.org
Physical Education (PE) is an important component in our schools’ curriculum, from primary to middle school. The overall objective of the subject is to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and attitude to lead a physically active and healthy lifestyle throughout their life.

I. 🎯 Course Expectations

You are expected to:

  • Give your best effort while participating in all activities.
  • Display respect and sportsmanship toward fellow classmates.
  • Learn rules and regulations for all activities.
  • Be properly dressed in P.E. shirt and P.E. shorts. Please put your name on ALL uniforms.

II. 📊 Grading

75%Participation
15%Written Unit Tests
10%P.E. Uniform Attendance

Written tests are given at the end of each unit. Study sheet will be posted in Google Classroom.

* Subject to change. A conduct grade will be given based on behavior in P.E. classes.

III. 🩹 Miscellaneous

Medical Conditions. Please inform your instructor immediately of any special medical conditions that may hinder you from participating.

Parent / Doctor Notes. Notes for non-participation due to sickness or injury must be given to the instructor at the beginning of class.

IV. 🏆 Team Sports and Activities

  • Q1: Volleyball · Throwing/Catching · Dodgeball
  • Q2: Basketball · Handball · Soccer · Cup Stacking
  • Q3: Baseball · Kickball · Golf · Jump Ropes
  • Q4: Football · Capture the Flag · Racket/Paddle
  • Q1 & Q4: Physical Fitness Testing / Mile Run
  • Games of Low Organization (year-round)
