📘 How It Works

Key idea: Pick the right endpoint for each rectangle.

Δx = (b − a)/n. List the x-values you need: left endpoints a, a + Δx, …; right endpoints start one step later; midpoints are halfway. Evaluate f at each, add, and multiply by Δx.
