Executive Summary

The Math 8 curriculum at Mary, Star of the Sea School meets or exceeds every domain of the CCSS Grade 8 standards and aligns fully with the IB MYP Year 3 Mathematics framework. IB (International Baccalaureate) MYP Year 3 Alignment

The IB Middle Years Programme organizes middle-school mathematics into four branches (Number; Algebra; Geometry & Trigonometry; Statistics & Probability) and emphasizes interdisciplinary applications across four MYP assessment criteria (A — Knowing & Understanding; B — Investigating Patterns; C — Communicating; D — Applying Mathematics in Real-Life Contexts). MSOS Math 8 aligns with — and in several branches exceeds — the MYP Year 3 framework:

IB-specific strengths of MSOS Math 8:

  • MYP Criterion D (Applying Mathematics in Real-Life Contexts) is exceptionally well served by the seven physics/science-integrated units. Most independent schools struggle to satisfy this criterion.
  • MYP Criterion B (Investigating Patterns) is embedded in the discovery-based worksheets (e.g., Scientific Notation calculator lab, Ohm's Law circuit modeling, Kepler's cube-square scaling).
  • MYP Criterion C (Communicating) is developed through the lab-report writing spiral shared with the STEM unit.
  • The curriculum's four-quarter thematic sequencing mirrors MYP unit planners: Number → Algebra → Geometry → Statistics & Applications.

Assessment Infrastructure

Every one of the 22 units provides three linked assessments accessible directly from the class website (starofthesea.org/classes/middle-school/):

  • Worksheet — multi-section skill-builder with reveal-answer keys per section (typically 5-8 sections).
  • Quiz — 10 targeted problems with hidden answer key for a quick check.
  • Unit Test — 20-problem comprehensive summative with formulas, worked examples, and answer key.

All 66 Grade 8 assessment files are print-friendly (auto-showing answer keys for teacher copies), grade-aware, and integrated with the unit navigation via "Worksheet →", "Quiz →", and "Unit Test →" buttons.
